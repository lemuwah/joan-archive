# GAP-M: F.L. Greene 1894 pp. 10–11 — RESOLVED (2026-09-04)

## Source
F.L. Greene, *Descendants of Joseph Greene of Westerly, R.I.* (1894), p. 10.  
[Internet Archive full text](https://archive.org/stream/descendantsofjos00gree/descendantsofjos00gree_djvu.txt)

## Verbatim Text (p. 10)

> "March 24th, 1682, he deeded his son Daniel 120 acres bordering on Allen's Harbor, and to his son James 60 acres adjoining, each of said sons to pay thirty shillings annually as long as their father or mother should live. The land was bounded partly by land of son John."

## What This Resolves

1. **C-3 (partially):** F.L. Greene's "father or mother should live" is a paraphrase of the manuscript's "him or his Wife or during her natural life." Same clause, modernized language. "Mother" = Joan as mother of the sons, NOT Joan's own mother.

2. **Acreage confirmed:** Daniel = 120 acres, James = 60 acres. Matches both the NK manuscript (Image 10) and Worth abstract.

3. **"Allen's Harbor"** — F.L. Greene places Daniel's land as "bordering on Allen's Harbor." This geographic detail is NOT in the Worth abstract. It may be from F.L. Greene's own knowledge of the area or from a source not cited. ⚠️ Unverified.

4. **"Land of son John"** — F.L. Greene says the land was bounded "partly by land of son John." Worth's James instrument says "Northerly by the land... John Greene." This confirms that John² (Lt. John, eldest son) already held adjacent land.

## What This Does NOT Resolve

- **C-2 (John Sr. death date):** This passage does not state when John Sr. died. The C-2 contradiction (Bates: "before September 25, 1685" vs. F.L. Greene references to 1686/1692) requires reading further in F.L. Greene pp. 10–11 for the death-date language.
