## Verbatim Extract (pp. 8–11, John of Quidnessett section)

### Page 8 (opening)
> In 1639, or shortly before, as appears from an affidavit given later in this sketch, Richard Smith, Sr., built a trading-post near the present village of Wickford, in North Kingstown, R.I. With him was living one John Greene, concerning whose parentage and previous history nothing is certainly known.

### Page 9 (political history)
> In 1663 Greene, in company with his friend Smith, declared himself in favor of being under the jurisdiction of Connecticut rather than of Rhode Island. His active opposition to the authority of Rhode Island may be accounted for by the fact that the title to the tract of land which he occupied, and probably owned, in Quidnesset neck, was based upon a purchase from the Indians made in direct opposition to an order of the Rhode Island General Court. If Rhode Island should prevail, his title would be worthless; while the success of Connecticut would confirm him in his possessions.
>
> His opposition was met by the following order:
> "Newport, 1664, May 5. Ordered, that a warrant goe from the Court to require John Greene Sen'r living at Narragansett to come before this Court."
>
> He was arrested a few days later and brought before the Court, where he replied with so much spirit as to give offence, but upon his asking pardon for his actions, it was declared, "The Court doe pass by his offence, and doe promise to theaforesayd John Greene all lawful protexion, and doe declare that he is still looked on as a freeman of the Collony."

### Page 9–10 (1671–1682)
> May 20th, 1671, having been assured of the title and peaceful possession of his home, he took the oath of allegiance to Rhode Island.
>
> January 1st, 1672, he and five others bought of the Indians a large tract of land in Narragansett. In 1672 and 1674 his name appears as witness to transfers of land.
>
> July 21st, 1679, he made an affidavit in support of the title of Richard Smith, Jr., to the lands in the vicinity of Wickford.

### Page 10 — THE KEY PASSAGE
> **March 24th, 1682, he deeded his son Daniel 120 acres bordering on Allen's Harbor, and to his son James 60 acres adjoining, each of said sons to pay thirty shillings annually as long as their father or mother should live. The land was bounded partly by land of son John.**
>
> **July 16th, 1686, he signed an address to the King.**
>
> **May 13th, 1692, he signed as witness to a deed. He probably died within the next four years, as his name does not appear in the list of Kingstown freemen of 1696.**
>
> **He left a will, as is shown by an allusion in a deed by his son Edward.**
>
> **His wife, in 1682, at the time of the transfer of land to his sons, was named Joan, but nothing further is known about her.**

### Page 10–11 — Children
> Children:
> I. John, Jr.², b. June 6, 1651; d. Oct. 6, 1729; m. Abigail D——; lived in East Greenwich and in that part of Warwick afterward set off as Coventry, and had eleven children.
>
> II. Daniel², b. ——; m. at Newport July 16, 1689 (perhaps a second marriage), to Rebecca Barrows; lived in North Kingstown, where he d. 1730; had seven children.
>
> III. James², b. 1655; d. 1728; m. (1) Elizabeth ——; (2) Ann ——; lived in North Kingstown, and had sons, John and James, of whom nothing more is known.
>
> **IV. Edward², b. ——; m. Mary Tibbitts, dau. of Henry; lived in North Kingstown. They had sons, as is shown by the will of their grandfather Tibbitts, but their names have not been preserved.**
>
> V. Benjamin², b. about 1665; m. Humility Coggeshall.

## 🚨 KEY FINDINGS

### 1. C-2: John Sr. Death Date — CONTRADICTION DOCUMENTED

| Source | Claim | Evidence Cited |

| **Bates 1918** | "He died before September 25, 1685" | Not stated |

### 2. H. Tibbits = Henry Tibbitts, Father-in-Law of Edward² Greene

F.L. Greene's children list states:
> "IV. Edward², b. ——; m. **Mary Tibbitts, dau. of Henry**"

Image 10 (Daniel instrument) has **"H Tibbits Mark"** as a witness.

**This means:** The witness on the March 1682 deed is **Henry Tibbitts** — the same Henry Tibbitts whose daughter Mary later married **Edward² Greene**, another of John's sons (not mentioned in the deed because he presumably received no land in this transaction).

The witness is the **future father-in-law** of one of the grantor's other sons. This is NOT a random neighbor witnessing a deed. This is a family-network connection.

**Seven-name convergence: CONFIRMED from independent source.** The Tibbitts thread now has manuscript evidence (Image 10) + genealogical corroboration (F.L. Greene).

### 3. Joan — "Nothing Further Is Known"
F.L. Greene 1894: "His wife, in 1682, at the time of the transfer of land to his sons, was named Joan, **but nothing further is known about her.**"

This is the earliest published admission of the archive silence around Joan. F.L. Greene, writing in 1894, had access to local records, family traditions, and the cooperation of "Mr. Paul Greene, of Adams Centre, N.Y." and "Joshua Greene Hull, of Gladbrook, Ia." — descendants who presumably would have known family oral history. Despite all of this, he found **nothing** about Joan beyond her name and the 1682 deed.

### 4. Allen's Harbor — Geographic Detail
F.L. Greene places Daniel's 120 acres as "bordering on Allen's Harbor." This geographic detail is NOT in the Worth abstract. It locates the home-place on the WATER — a harbor, not just inland acreage. Combined with the deed's "Easterly by quidnesett harbour" (Worth, James instrument), this confirms the Greene home-place had waterfront access.

### 5. Children List — Edward² Confirmed
F.L. Greene lists five children: John Jr.², Daniel², James², **Edward²**, Benjamin². This matches Bates's list (adding Sarah² / Thomas Flounders). Edward² is the "missing" son — not in the March 1682 deed — who later references John's will in one of his own deeds.

### 6. Benjamin² — "born about 1665"
F.L. Greene dates Benjamin² to "about 1665" and says he married Humility Coggeshall. The full Benjamin entry (pp. 11–14) shows him as "of North Kingstown and afterward of East Greenwich," freeman 1696, deputy 1698–1703, died winter 1718–19. This is the same Benjamin whose name appears in Image 10/11 as "Beni Green of Kingstown" buying the Havens land.
