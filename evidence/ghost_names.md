# Ghost Name Register — Suspended Names With No Evidence Trail

**Status:** ACTIVE — created 2026-09-09 per owner directive
**Purpose:** Names that appear in the archive's orbit **without any located evidence trail** — no primary record, no named secondary source with a page citation, only unsourced compiled lists or broken references. These names are **suspended, not deleted.**

## The rules (owner directive, 2026-09-09)

1. **We do not invent narrative.** A name with no evidence trail does not get a biography, a family role, or a place in a list of persons.
2. **We do not minimize names.** Suspension is not erasure. Every ghost name stays on this register permanently, fully visible, with everything we know about where it came from.
3. **Each name is kept SEPARATE.** We do not know whether any two names — here or elsewhere in the archive — are the same person. No merging, no collapsing, no "probably the same as" without a document. Possible-identity questions are recorded as OPEN.
4. **We write what we find, and we test all theories.** Every ghost name remains a **live search target**. A ghost graduates off this register only when a record with a date, place, and source anchor is located — or when a scoped negative search is logged.
5. Ghost names may not appear on public pages or in people-page person lists. They may appear in search logs, this register, and research working notes, always with their ⚫ GHOST status.

**Tag:** ⚫ GHOST — SUSPENDED, NO EVIDENCE TRAIL (distinct from ⚠️ UNVERIFIED, which has a claimed source not yet checked, and from 🔴 ELIMINATED).
