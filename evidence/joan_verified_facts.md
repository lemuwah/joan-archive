## Fact 1

**Statement:** Joan is named as wife of John Greene in a March 1682 land deed.  
**Source:** Worth abstract, *Rhode Island Land Evidences* Vol. I, p. 122 (Abstracts, 1921 ed.)  
**Tier:** 1 — Primary abstract  
**Status:** 🟢 VERIFIED  
**Notes:** Worth describes John Greene of Quidnessett conveying land to sons Daniel and James. Joan is named as "Joane Greene, his wife." The manuscript original (FamilySearch DGS 008204949, images 9–12) has not been read by this project.

---

## Fact 2

**Statement:** A life annuity of 30 shillings per year is reserved to her.  
**Source:** Worth abstract, *Rhode Island Land Evidences* Vol. I, p. 122  
**Tier:** 1 — Primary abstract  
**Status:** 🟢 VERIFIED  
**Notes:** Worth records a 30-shilling life annuity reserved to Joan. The NK manuscript reads "dureing her naturall life" (per partial transcription). Whether the annuity reverts to Joan's mother or to the sons' parents is ⚫ SUSPENDED — see `contradictions/mother-clause-wording.md`.

## Fact 3

**Statement:** No other record of Joan has been located.  
**Source:** Methodological — negative search result  
**Tier:** Methodological  
**Status:** 🟢 VERIFIED (as of Sep 2026)  
**Notes:** No birth, baptism, marriage, death, burial, probate, church, court, or land record has been found for Joan outside the March 1682 deed. The Pawtuxet deed (May 1682) citation was invalidated — see `CORRECTIONS.md` #1. The negative search log is at `primary_sources/NEGATIVE_LOG.md`.
