What we haven't searched yet — and how you can help.

The Joan Archive is an open-access research project. The most valuable contributions are not interpretations — they are searches. Every repository checked, every null result logged, narrows the field for everyone.

Highest Priority: The Manuscript

FamilySearch DGS 008204949, images 9–12

The original manuscript of the March 1682 deed. This is the single most decisive unexamined source. It could resolve every suspended claim in the archive: consent, mark, mother clause, co-grantor status.

HIGHEST PRIORITY — If you have access to this microfilm or can visit a FamilySearch center, this is the contribution that changes everything.

Repositories Not Yet Searched

Under Law 5 (No Jurisdictional Assumption), the archive cannot eliminate any hypothesis without checking the relevant record types. These repositories have been identified but not searched:

For Models A & B (Narragansett / English)

  • First Baptist Church, Providence — 1640s–1680s membership rolls (fragmentary)
  • Newport Historical Society — church records
  • Massachusetts State Archives, Boston — court records 1637–1645 (pre-RI records for John Greene)

For Model C (Irish origin)

  • Irish parish records — Y-DNA points to Irish Sea cluster

For Model D (Indentured servant)

  • RI, MA, and Plymouth indenture records

For Model E (Enslaved person)

  • Post-1676 RI enslavement records

For Model F (Widow of prior marriage)

  • RI probate records pre-1682

Overlapping jurisdiction (Law 5)

  • UK National Archives, Kew — Colonial Office CO 1/XX series
  • Connecticut State Library — colonial land records
  • Plymouth County Court Records — 1637–1692
  • Dutch National Archives, The Hague — WIC records re: Richard Smith

The full negative evidence log is on GitHub: NEGATIVE_LOG.md

How to Contribute

  • Search a repository from the list above and report what you find — including null results.
  • Open a GitHub issue at github.com/lemuwah/joan-archive/issues with your findings.
  • Email the archive owner if you prefer private communication (see About).
  • Correct an error — the archive documents its mistakes publicly. See CORRECTIONS.md.

A Note for Indigenous Contributors

If you are from a Narragansett or other Indigenous research background, your input carries distinct and higher weight — not folded into general feedback. The archive recognizes that colonial records are partial instruments (see Epistemology.md) and that descriptions of Narragansett governance on this site are derived from non-Indigenous academic sources.

Contact has been initiated with the Tomaquag Museum. As of September 2026, no formal review has occurred. A private channel is available for Indigenous contributors who prefer not to use GitHub.
