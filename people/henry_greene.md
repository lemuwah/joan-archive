# Henry² Greene

**Status:** 🟡 SECONDARY — named by Bates and F.L. Greene only  
**Role in archive:** Son of John Sr. and Joan. Witness on the 1672 Fones Purchase deed (by mark).

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Bates 1918 pp. 169–171:** Listed among John Sr.'s children.
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "Henry² b. ——."
- **1672 Fones Purchase deed:** "Henry Greene his marke" — a witness. (This is a different generation's document — connects the Greene family to Narragansett sachem-land transactions.)

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ Birth date, marriage, children, death
- ❌ Where he lived after 1682
- ❌ Whether he received any land from John Sr. outside the 1682 deed

## How Henry Could Surface Joan

- **He witnessed the 1672 Fones deed naming Anashuecot.** Henry was present when a Narragansett sachem conveyed land. If Joan IS connected to Narragansett leadership, Henry's presence at that signing takes on new meaning.
- **His own records.** If Henry had a will, probate, or land deeds, they might mention Joan.

## Where to Look

1. NK/Kingstown/RI land and probate records — "Henry Greene"
2. Five-John Firewall applies — distinguish from other Henrys
3. 1672 Fones Purchase deed original — is Henry's mark distinctive?
