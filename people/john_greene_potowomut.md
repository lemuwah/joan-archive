## What We Know

The archive previously held this page deliberately blank. That was the correct posture. The first search pass has now produced findings, all tertiary, that **weaken the case for this being a distinct 17th-century John at all.**

### Finding 1 — "of Potowomut" is primarily James Greene's epithet, not a John's

Multiple independent tertiary sources attach the designation "of Potowomut" to **James Greene**, son of Surgeon John Greene and Joane Tattershall, baptized at St. Thomas' Church, Salisbury, England, 21 June 1626.

> "JAMES GREENE 'of Potowomut' (John), was baptized at St. Thomas' Church, Salisbury, England, June 21, 1626, and came with his parents to New England..."

🌐 TERTIARY — RootsWeb Fitchett genealogy; beach-mcgee.com PDF extract of *The Greene Family History England to America*; greeneandmiranda.wordpress.com. These are likely all reproducing the same underlying compiled genealogy, so they are **not independent corroboration** — they are one claim appearing in three places.

One tertiary source states James Greene "became the first Englishman to actually settle in Potowomut." 🌐 TERTIARY — storyofrhodeisland.com

### Finding 2 — The "John of Potowomut" who appears in compiled genealogies was born 1685

WikiTree records a John Greene, son of **James** and Elizabeth Greene, born 30 September 1685 in Potowomut, died 8 December 1757 in Warwick, buried in the Surgeon John Greene Lot in Warwick. A second WikiTree entry gives a John Greene of Potowomut 14 April 1691 – 8 December 1757. 🌐 TERTIARY — wikitree.com/wiki/Greene-739, Greene-1980

The two entries disagree on the birth year (1685 vs 1691) while sharing a death date, which is itself a sign of compiled-tree contamination.

**Either way, a man born in the 1680s cannot be a peer of the five 17th-century Johns**, and cannot be a candidate for Joan's husband, who was already conveying land to adult sons in 1682.

### Finding 3 — Potowomut Neck was acquired in 1654, and it is Surgeon-line territory

Potowomut Neck was purchased from the sachem **Taccomanan** by a group of Warwick men in 1654. 🌐 TERTIARY — riliberator.com; storyofrhodeisland.com

Burial of the 1685/1691 John "in the Surgeon John Greene Lot in Warwick" places this Potowomut line in the **Surgeon's** family, not the Quidnessett family. 🌐 TERTIARY

### What this means for the Five John Firewall

If Findings 1–3 survive primary verification, "Potowomut John" is not a sixth John to be separated from the others. It is a **later-generation descendant of the Surgeon's line**, and the epithet properly belongs to James.

⚠️ **This parallels a structural problem elsewhere in the task queue.** `people/john_greene_warwick.md` establishes that Major John Greene of Warwick **is** "John Greene of Occupasuetuxet" — the same man under two designations. Both `john_greene_potowomut.md` and `john_greene_occupessuatuxet.md` therefore appear to be **locality labels from the Surgeon's line** rather than distinct unresolved Johns. Flagged, not resolved. See `TASK_QUEUE.md` Task 5.

## What We Don't Know

1. **Whether any 17th-century John Greene was ever styled "of Potowomut."** Not established either way. All located instances of the epithet attach to James, or to a John born in the 1680s.
2. **Which of the 1685 / 1691 birth years is correct** for James's son John, or whether the compiled trees have merged two men.
3. **Whether "Potowomut" entered this archive's John list from a primary record or from a compiler's label.** This is the decisive question and it is unanswered. The original reason the slot was created is not documented on this page.
4. **The full 1654 Potowomut Neck purchase party.** Whether any John Greene is named among the Warwick purchasers is unverified — the tertiary sources name only the sachem Taccomanan and "some Warwick men."
5. **Taccomanan's identity and kin position**, and how the 1654 Potowomut transaction relates to the 1642 Occupasuetuxet purchase from Miantonomi and the 1672 Fones Purchase from Anashuecot. Three Narragansett grantors, three Greene-associated tracts, no comparative analysis done.
