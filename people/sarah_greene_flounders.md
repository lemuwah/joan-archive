## What We Know

### 1668 Rhode Island Court Record (Primary)
- **"Sarah Greene alias Flounders"** and **"Sarah Greene alias Sarah Flounders"** — fornication charge.
- Her father **"John Greene"** appeared and admitted the charge, paying a 40-shilling fine.
- This establishes both her parentage (daughter of a John Greene) and her association with Thomas Flounders by 1668.

### Thomas Flounders
- **May 4, 1668:** Thomas signed a Wickford/Narragansett petition seeking Connecticut protection.
- **July 3, 1663:** Thomas signed an earlier petition with Narragansett inhabitants and proprietors.
- **July 11, 1670:** Thomas killed Walter House in Wickford.
- **November 2, 1670:** Thomas executed at Newport.

### After Execution
- 🚨 **Edward Greenman and John Greene of Newport** had charge of Thomas's estate for the widow and child.
- Edward Greenman = same witness on the 1682 deed.
- No complete inventory, will, or distribution located.

### The Estate Petition — full mechanism (Austin, FLOUNDERS entry, 2026-09-09)
Austin's abstract of the colony record adds the operative detail the archive lacked: upon execution, **Thomas's estate was forfeited to the King**. Edward Greenman and **John Greene, of Newport** petitioned the Assembly (26 Oct 1670) asking that the estate go to "his late wife **Sarah**" after the charges of the execution were paid. The Assembly — quoted as "commiserating the solitary and poor estate and condition of Sarah, the late wife of the aforenamed Thomas Flounders" — granted the petition; the widow received "all bedding and household stuff, a cow and hog, together with the corn." Austin also records the killing's circumstances: House, holding up his arm, fell backward and struck his head on a rafter — Flounders was in the shop; the death may have been a fall, yet the execution followed. 📚 SECONDARY (Austin's abstract; the Assembly record original not yet pulled). **Firewall note:** the petitioner is styled "of Newport" — this feeds both the Newport/Quidnessett alias question and the which-John paternity question; it does not settle either.

### From Bates 1918 p. 78
- Sarah² listed as child of John Greene and wife Joan, married Thomas Flounders about 1668.

## 🚨 THE FIVE-JOHN FIREWALL PROBLEM

Which John Greene appeared in court for Sarah in 1668?

- **Smith's Castle study:** Identifies her father as John Greene Jr., "Major Greene," of Potowomut/Warwick.
- **RIHS Collections (Bates):** Lists her under "John Greene of Newport and Narragansett" — i.e., Quidnessett John.
- **The court record itself:** Says only "John Greene" — no qualifier.

**If Sarah's father is Quidnessett John:**
- Sarah is Joan's daughter ✓
- Sarah's fornication charge (1668) means Joan had a teenage/young adult daughter by 1668
- Joan was married to John by at least the early 1650s
- Sarah's child (born ~1668–69) is Joan's grandchild
- Edward Greenman managing Thomas's estate for the widow = consistent with the Greenman-Greene network seen in 1682

**If Sarah's father is Warwick John (Major Greene):**
- Sarah is NOT Joan's daughter
- She belongs to the Surgeon's line entirely
- Bates made a Five-John error

⚠️ **UNRESOLVED. Both attributions exist in published sources. Neither cites an original record that disambiguates which John.**
