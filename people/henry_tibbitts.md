### From Henry Tibbitts's Will (1708, proved 1713)
**Source:** Multiple independent abstracts — Austin *Genealogical Dictionary* p. 202, Beaman pp. 139–142, *RI Genealogical Register* vol. 4 no. 4 p. 320, Tanner *Genealogy* p. 13.

⚠️ **Original document fire-damaged** (NK records fire). Multiple independent abstracts exist.

Bequests:
- **Sarah Tibbitts** — wife; co-executor. Half of land (while a widow), half of orchard and housing.
- **George Tibbitts** — son; co-executor. Other half of land; Sarah's share after her death; care of his mother.
- **John Tibbitts** — son. Land in East Greenwich where he lived.
- **Henry Tibbitts Jr.** — son (deceased by 1708). His widow Rebecca and children provided for.
- **Mary Greene** — 🚨 **eldest daughter; wife of Edward Greene "of Aquidneset."** £20 4s.
- **Martha Stanton** — daughter; wife of Benjamin Stanton. £20.
- **Anne Fones** — 🚨 **granddaughter (daughter of Samuel Fones).** £10. FONES CONNECTION.
- **William Tanner** — grandson. Land purchased from Thomas Stanton of Stonington; five loads of hay.
- **Edward Greene's sons** — 🚨 **Joan's grandsons.** Excluded from the grandson land distribution because they were "provided for."

Estate inventory: £64 11s.

### From Other Records
- **Dec 1661:** Henry married Sarah Stanton at Kingston (Quaker marriage record).
- **20 May 1671:** Henry Tibbits and Edward Green appear in Quidnessett court/land material.

## What This Means for Joan

### 🚨 The will MENTIONS but does NOT NAME Joan.

The will says Edward Greene's sons were "provided for" — meaning they already had land or income. The 1682 deed's life-estate clause paid Joan 30 shillings annually from Daniel and James. If Joan was alive in 1708, the Tibbitts sons' provision might include land that was NOT subject to Joan's life-estate (because Edward's land came through his father's will, not the 1682 deed).

But the will does NOT say "widow Greene" or "Joan" or "mother of Edward." Joan may have been dead by 1708. Or she may have been alive but simply not Tibbitts's responsibility.

### 🚨 The Fones Connection

Anne Fones, granddaughter of Henry Tibbitts, daughter of Samuel Fones. The Fones Purchase (1672) is the document where Anashuecot appears. Now we have a FAMILY connection between the Tibbitts/Greene network and the Fones family. This needs investigation.

### 🚨 The Stanton Connection

Sarah Stanton married Henry Tibbitts. Martha Tibbitts married Benjamin Stanton. William Tanner received land purchased from Thomas Stanton of Stonington. The Stanton family was deeply involved in Narragansett affairs — Robert Stanton was an interpreter.
