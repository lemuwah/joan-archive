# Henry Tibbitts (H. Tibbits)

**Status:** 🟢 IDENTIFIED — confirmed from F.L. Greene 1894 and Image 10 witness block  
**Role in archive:** Witness on the March 24, 1682 deed. Father-in-law of Edward² Greene.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 (NK Land Records):** "H Tibbits Mark" — one of two witnesses (with Edward Greenman).
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** Henry Tibbitts, whose daughter Mary married Edward² Greene. "They had sons, as is shown by the will of their grandfather Tibbitts."
- **Seven-name convergence:** Tibbitts is connected to Greene, Greenman, Smith, Fones, and Absolome networks.

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ His birth/death dates
- ❌ His wife's name
- ❌ Where he lived (Quidnessett? Kingstown? elsewhere?)
- ❌ His other children besides Mary
- ❌ The date of the Tibbitts will F.L. Greene references

## How Tibbitts Could Surface Joan

- **His will.** F.L. Greene says it names Edward and Mary's sons (Joan's grandchildren). It might also name Joan directly — as "the widow Greene" or "mother of Edward" or reference obligations to her.
- **His proximity.** He witnessed the 1682 deed. He was physically present when John transferred land. He knew Joan. His own deeds, court appearances, and neighbor interactions could reference the Greene household.
- **His daughter Mary.** Mary Tibbitts married into Joan's family. Mary's own life — baptisms, land, church — might reference Joan as mother-in-law.

## Where to Look

1. Henry Tibbitts will — RI probate records (NK, Kingstown, or Washington County)
2. NK/Kingstown land records — Tibbitts as grantor/grantee
3. RI vital records — Tibbitts family entries
4. Quaker meeting records (if applicable — many Narragansett-country families were Quaker)
