# Edward² Greene

**Status:** 🟡 SECONDARY — documented from F.L. Greene 1894 and web search; no primary source read by archive  
**Role in archive:** Son of John Sr. and Joan. NOT a grantee on the 1682 deed. His 1695 deed quotes John's lost will.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "Edward² b. ——; m. Mary Tibbitts, dau. of Henry."
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "He left a will, as is shown by an allusion in a deed by his son Edward."
- **March 8, 1695 deed to George Vaughan:** 10 acres in East Greenwich. Reported language: "fell to me by will of my honored father, lately deceased." (Source: Austin p. 86 / Bates — NOT read directly by archive)
- **Sept 4, 1697 deed to George Vaughan:** 90 acres in East Greenwich. Language: "given by father."
- **1695 deed to George Havens:** Edward also sold land to Havens — same Havens who bought from Benjamin².
- **1682 deed witness H. Tibbits:** Henry Tibbitts, Edward's future father-in-law, witnessed the 1682 deed transferring land to Daniel and James — but NOT to Edward.

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ Why Edward received NO land in the 1682 deed (was he away? disinherited? too young?)
- ❌ Whether "lately deceased" in 1695 is literal or formulaic
- ❌ What the lost will actually said about Joan
- ❌ Whether Edward and Mary Tibbitts's children knew Joan
- ❌ Where the 1695/1697 Vaughan deeds are physically recorded

## How Edward Could Surface Joan

- **His 1695 deed.** It quotes John's will. The FULL DEED TEXT might contain more language about the inheritance — including obligations to Joan or mention of her status (alive? dead? provided for?).
- **Henry Tibbitts's will.** F.L. Greene says Edward and Mary "had sons, as is shown by the will of their grandfather Tibbitts." That will names Edward's children — and might reference Joan if she was still alive.
- **Mary Tibbitts.** If Mary's family (the Tibbitts/Tibbetts) had their own records, Joan might appear as "mother-in-law" or "the widow Greene."

## Where to Look

1. NK Land Records — Edward Greene → George Vaughan deeds (1695, 1697)
2. NK Land Records — Edward Greene → George Havens deed (1695)
3. East Greenwich land records — both Vaughan deeds may be recorded there instead of NK
4. Henry Tibbitts will — RI probate records, NK or Kingstown
5. Austin's *Genealogical Dictionary* p. 86 — verify "lately deceased" quote directly
