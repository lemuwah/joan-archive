## What We Know

### From F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10
- "Edward² b. ——; m. Mary Tibbitts, dau. of Henry."
- "He left a will, as is shown by an allusion in a deed by his son Edward."

### From Edward's Deeds (not read directly — reported in secondary sources)
- **March 8, 1695 to George Vaughan:** 10 acres in East Greenwich. "Fell to me by will of my honored father, lately deceased."
- **Sept 4, 1697 to George Vaughan:** 90 acres in East Greenwich. "Given by father."
- **1695 to George Havens:** Edward also sold land to Havens.

### From Image 10 (Primary)
- 1682 deed witness: "H Tibbits Mark" = Henry Tibbitts, Edward's future father-in-law.

### From Tibbitts Will (1708)
- 🚨 **"Mary Greene, eldest daughter, wife of Edward Greene of Aquidneset"** — confirms Edward lived at Aquidneset (= Quidnessett) and was married to Mary Tibbitts.
- 🚨 **"Edward Greene's sons"** excluded from grandson land distribution because "provided for." These are Joan's grandsons.

### From Court Records
- **22 Apr 1700:** Edward Green appears in a Kingstown court enquiry involving a riot and prisoner rescue, alongside Henry Tibbitts Jr. and John Tibbetts.
- **20 May 1671:** "Edward Green" appears in Quidnessett court/land material with Henry Tibbits.
