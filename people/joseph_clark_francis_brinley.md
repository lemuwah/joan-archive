# Joseph Clark & Francis Brinley

**Status:** 🟡 SECONDARY — cited by Bates 1918 but not read directly  
**Role in archive:** Parties to the September 25, 1685 Jamestown deed that mentions "John Greene, sr., of Narragansett, now deceased."

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Bates 1918 p. 74:** "September 25, 1685, Joseph Clark, above, of Newport, sold the land which was bounded south by Caleb Carr's land which was formerly in possession of 'John Greene, sr., of Narragansett, now deceased,' to Francis Brinley."
- **This is the primary evidence for Bates's death date claim.**

## How Clark/Brinley Could Surface Joan

- **The deed itself.** Bates says John's land is described in this deed. The FULL DEED TEXT might mention who now holds that land — if it's Joan ("his widow"), that's a second documentary appearance.
- **Caleb Carr's land.** If Carr's deed also references Greene land, there may be additional boundary descriptions mentioning Joan or her interest.
- **Brinley's later deeds.** If Brinley sold the same land, his deed's provenance chain might reference the Greene family.

## Where to Look

1. **Jamestown Town Records** — the September 25, 1685 Clark-to-Brinley deed. This is the #1 priority for C-2 resolution.
2. Jamestown town clerk's office or RI State Archives
3. FamilySearch — Jamestown deed books may be digitized
4. Caleb Carr's land records — boundary descriptions
