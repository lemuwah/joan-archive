# James² Greene

**Status:** 🟢 DOCUMENTED — confirmed from manuscript (Image 10) and secondary sources  
**Role in archive:** Son of John Sr. and Joan. Grantee of the March 24, 1682 deed.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 (NK Land Records, DGS 008204949):** Named as grantee of 60 acres. Obligated to pay 30 shillings annually to John Sr. or Joan during their lifetimes.
- **Bates 1918 p. 76:** One of two sons receiving Quidnessett home-place land.
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "James² b. ——; m. ——."
- **Feb 15, 1695/6 deed:** "James Greene his brother, 18¾ acres, bounded in part by Edward Greene his brother." Record badly damaged.
- **Post-1700 deed:** James Greene and Elizabeth Greene (his wife) to John Corey.
