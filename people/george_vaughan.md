# George Vaughan

**Status:** 🟡 SECONDARY — named in Edward² deeds but not read directly  
**Role in archive:** Buyer of Edward²'s inherited land. The deeds to Vaughan quote John Sr.'s lost will.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **March 8, 1695 deed from Edward²:** 10 acres in East Greenwich. Language: "fell to me by will of my honored father, lately deceased."
- **Sept 4, 1697 deed from Edward²:** 90 acres in East Greenwich. Language: "given by father."

## How Vaughan Could Surface Joan

- **The deeds themselves.** We have REPORTED language but haven't READ the deeds. The full text might contain more — recitals of the will, mention of Joan's life-estate interest, restrictions or reservations.
- **Vaughan's own records.** If Vaughan later sold the same land, his deed's provenance chain might reference Joan.

## Where to Look

1. East Greenwich land records — Vaughan deeds (March 1695, Sept 1697)
2. NK Land Records (DGS 008204949) — may also be recorded here
3. Austin's *Genealogical Dictionary* p. 86 — verify the quoted language
