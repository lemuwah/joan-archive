# Benjamin² Greene

**Status:** 🟢 DOCUMENTED — first independent manuscript appearance on Image 10 bottom  
**Role in archive:** Son of John Sr. and Joan. Buyer in the George Havens deed.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 bottom (NK Land Records, DGS 008204949):** "paid by Beni Green of Kingstown" — buyer in the George Havens deed, paying thirty pounds.
- **Bates 1918 pp. 169–171:** Listed among John Sr.'s children.
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "Benjamin² b. ——."

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ Birth date
- ❌ Marriage — did he marry? Who?
- ❌ Children
- ❌ When he died
- ❌ What land he bought from Havens and where it was
- ❌ Any independent appearances beyond the Havens deed

## How Benjamin Could Surface Joan

- **His land purchase.** If the Havens deed describes the land as formerly belonging to "John Greene Sr.," it connects back to the family estate and potentially to Joan's life-estate interest.
- **His will or probate.** If Benjamin married and had children, his will might reference "my mother" or inherited obligations.
- **The Havens connection.** George Havens sold to both Benjamin AND Edward. Havens's own records could mention the Greene family collectively.

## Where to Look

1. Image 10 bottom — full read of the George Havens deed
2. NK Land Records grantor/grantee index — "Benjamin Greene" or "Beni Green"
3. NK/Kingstown probate records
4. Kingstown tax lists, freemen lists — is Benjamin on the 1696 list that John Sr. is absent from?
