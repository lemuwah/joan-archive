## What We Know

- **Image 10 (NK Land Records, DGS 008204949):** Named as grantee of 120 acres bordered by Allen's Harbor. Obligated to pay 30 shillings annually to John Sr. or Joan during their lifetimes.
- **Bates 1918 p. 76:** One of two sons receiving Quidnessett home-place land.
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "Daniel² b. about 1647; m. ——; left land in Kingstown."
- **1676 Newport court martial (Hough 1858 p. 181):** A "Daniel Green" appears as witness. Age ~29 if b. 1647.
- **Feb 15, 1695/6 deed:** "James Greene his brother, 18¾ acres, bounded in part by Edward Greene his brother." Daniel may be involved — record badly damaged.
