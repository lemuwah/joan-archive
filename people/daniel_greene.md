# Daniel² Greene

**Status:** 🟢 DOCUMENTED — confirmed from manuscript (Image 10) and secondary sources  
**Role in archive:** Son of John Sr. and Joan. Grantee of the March 24, 1682 deed. Witness at the 1676 court martial.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 (NK Land Records, DGS 008204949):** Named as grantee of 120 acres bordered by Allen's Harbor. Obligated to pay 30 shillings annually to John Sr. or Joan during their lifetimes.
- **Bates 1918 p. 76:** One of two sons receiving Quidnessett home-place land.
- **F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10:** "Daniel² b. about 1647; m. ——; left land in Kingstown."
- **1676 Newport court martial (Hough 1858 p. 181):** A "Daniel Green" appears as witness. Age ~29 if b. 1647.
- **Feb 15, 1695/6 deed:** "James Greene his brother, 18¾ acres, bounded in part by Edward Greene his brother." Daniel may be involved — record badly damaged.

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ His wife's name (F.L. Greene says "m. ——")
- ❌ Whether he had children
- ❌ When he died
- ❌ Whether his wife's family is identifiable
- ❌ Whether he had any contact with Joan after John Sr.'s death

## How Daniel Could Surface Joan

- **His deed payments.** Daniel owed 30 shillings annually to Joan after John died. If he recorded those payments, or if any dispute arose, Joan appears in the record.
- **His will or probate.** If Daniel's will mentions obligations to his mother, Joan is named.
- **His wife.** If Daniel married a Narragansett or mixed-heritage woman, it strengthens the indigenous-connection hypothesis for the family.
- **His witness at the court martial (1676).** He was present at the trial of Awaswin/Anaftiawin — a Narragansett sachem — 6 years before Joan appears in the deed. Why was he there?

2. NK/Kingstown probate records 1690–1730
3. Kingstown town meeting records — did Daniel serve in any capacity?
4. RI Colonial Records — any court appearances
5. Church/vital records — marriage, burial
