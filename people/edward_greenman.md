# Edward Greenman

**Status:** 🟢 DOCUMENTED — appears on Image 10 and in Bates  
**Role in archive:** Witness on the March 24, 1682 deed. Also connected to a 1647 Newport land acknowledgment with John Sr.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 (NK Land Records):** Named as witness alongside H. Tibbits.
- **Bates 1918 p. 76:** "On February 4, 1682, Edward Greenman acknowledged, for himself and brother David, the deed he had given Greene to land in Newport in 1647."
- **1682 deed:** Greenman witnessed the home-place conveyance to Daniel and James.

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ His relationship to John Sr. beyond the land transactions
- ❌ His family
- ❌ Whether he knew Joan personally

## How Greenman Could Surface Joan

- **The 1647 Newport land.** This is the earliest documented transaction involving John Sr. Full text might contain names or details relevant to Joan.
