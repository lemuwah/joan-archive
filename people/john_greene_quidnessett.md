## Identity

- **Designation:** John Greene of Quidnessett (Aquidneset); later styled John Greene, Senior
- **Born:** c. 1606, probably England; exact place and date unproved
- **Immigration:** Traditionally said to have arrived 1635, possibly on the *Matthew*; traditional, not proven
- **Died:** After 24 March 1681/82 (last documented appearance in the home-place deed). Death "about 1695" is a traditional date, not firmly documented. A deed of **25 Sept 1685** refers to "John Greene Sr. now deceased" (Bates 1918–19); if confirmed against the primary source, this would narrow death to between March 1682 and Sept 1685
- **Wife:** **Joan Greene** — maiden name unknown. "Joan Beggarly" is La Mance (1904) only. **Alice Beggarly's death before 1682 (proved via Benedict Arnold letter + Winthrop Papers) makes the La Mance identification timeline-impossible.** See `research/legal_danger_framework.md` for why the maiden name question is more complex than genealogists assumed
- **Marriage:** Probably c. 1642; no marriage record located

**Sources:** Bates, *RIHS Collections* XI–XII (1918–19); F.L. Greene 1894 (p. 10); 1682 deed (NK Land Evidence)

## Key Land Records

### The 1672 Fones Purchase

On **1 January 1671/72**, John Greene joined **John Fones, Henry Tibbits, John Andrews, John Briggs, and Thomas Waterman** in purchasing from the Narragansett sachem **Anashuecot** (also Awashowat, Awashouse — see `notes/2026-08-28_anashuecot_phonetic_variants.md`) a large tract at Quidnessett, known as the **Devil's Foot** or **Fones Purchase**.

**Source:** Fones Record (Arnold transcription / RI State Archives). Confirmed on Fones Record p. 90 by RI State Archives.

### The 1682 Home-Place Partition Deed

Dated **24 March 1681/82**. Conveys portions of Quidnessett land to sons:
- **Daniel Greene:** ~120 acres bordering Allen's Harbor
- **James Greene:** ~60 acres adjoining Daniel's land
- Each son to pay **30 shillings annually** during the lifetime of John or Joan
- Land bounded in part by land of their brother **John**
- **Life-estate clause:** "as long as their father or mother should live" / "or to her mother if she survive"

James described as the **"natural son of John and Joan Greene"** (17th-century usage = biological son).

**Source:** NK Land Evidence; F.L. Greene 1894 (p. 10)

### The 1682 Pawtuxet Consent

Dated **19 May 1682**. Joan gives her **"free & voluntary consent"** to the sale of Pawtuxet land rights to William Carpenter for £10. Joan signs **"her marke."** Witnesses: John Wickes, Samuel Gorton.

**Source:** *Records of RI & Providence Plantations*, Vol. VII, pp. 177–178

### East Greenwich Proprietorship (1679)

"John Greene, Sr." drew the **ninth house lot** in the first ten-acre division and a **ninety-acre farm** in the second division among the original East Greenwich proprietors.

**Source:** Bates 1918–19

### 1679 Affidavit

John Greene's own affidavit stating he had lived at Cocumscussoc for **"forty years and more"** — placing his arrival c. 1639–1642, consistent with settling alongside Richard Smith.

**Source:** Primary affidavit, cited in Bates and F.L. Greene
