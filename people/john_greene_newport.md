## Identity

- **Designation:** John Greene of Newport / Green End
- **Born:** Unknown — no reliable birth date, birthplace, or parentage established
- **Died:** Unknown as a conclusively separate individual. A death c. 1694–1695 is sometimes assigned but belongs to the disputed Newport/Quidnessett identification
- **Wife:** **Mary Jefferay** given by Austin — daughter of William and Mary (Gould) Jefferay, born 20 March 1642. **This attribution is disputed** and may be misassigned generationally

**Source:** Austin, *Genealogical Dictionary of RI*; WikiTree Greene Farm Archaeology Project analysis

## Children (uncertain)

**Austin's version:** One recorded child — John Greene (later purchased 60 acres at Shrewsbury, NJ, in 1716; married Sarah Peckham then Sarah Parrott; d. Newport 1740)

**Bates's alternative:** Traces the Green End line through Henry Greene and attributes five children (John, Sarah, Henry, William, Mary) to a later-generation John

⚠️ **Austin and Bates may have confused second- and third-generation Johns, their wives, and their land transactions.** The children list is unreliable without independent verification.

## Freeman Records

- Appears in the **1655 list of Newport freemen**
- A later John Greene of Green End was admitted a Newport freeman in **1694** — this should NOT automatically be conflated with the earlier man

**Source:** *Records of the Colony of RI and Providence Plantations*, Vol. I

## Offices Held

- Commissioner from Newport to the General Court — 1655, 1656, 1657, 1658, 1660

**Source:** *Records of the Colony of RI*

## Key Land Records

- **1647:** Purchased **22 acres at Green End** from David and Edward Greenman (deed acknowledged 4 Feb 1682)
- **1651:** Purchased **80 acres from Walter Cunnigrave** at Green End
- **1651:** Purchased **100 acres from Caleb Carr near Allen's Harbor, Quidnessett** ⚠️ This is the transaction that creates the Newport/Quidnessett overlap
- **1656:** Purchased **one-fortieth share in Conanicut Island**
- **1661:** Sold half the Conanicut share to John Sanford and half to Caleb Carr

**Source:** Newport land evidence records; Bates 1918–19

## The Newport–Quidnessett Question

Bates argued that this man and John Greene of Quidnessett were **the same person**, based on:
- The 1651 Quidnessett land purchase (100 acres from Caleb Carr near Allen's Harbor)
- Movement from Newport to Quidnessett c. 1661–1663
- Overlapping property chains

If Bates is correct, this profile and the Quidnessett profile describe the same individual at different stages of his life — a Newport commissioner who moved to Quidnessett, married Joan, and became a Fones Purchase proprietor.

If Bates is wrong, they are two separate men who happened to operate in overlapping geography.

**Per Law 1:** Held open. The profiles are maintained separately until resolution.

## What Distinguishes Him from the Other Johns

1. **1655 Newport freeman list** — same year as Warwick John's Warwick roll, proving two different men in two different towns
2. **Newport commissionerships** 1655–1660 — Warwick John was commissioner from Warwick in overlapping years
3. **Green End property** — a Newport neighborhood, not Occupasuetuxet or Quidnessett
4. **No military title** — Warwick John was "Major"; this man is never styled with a rank
5. **Wife Mary Jefferay (disputed)** — not Ann Almy (Warwick), not Joan (Quidnessett)
