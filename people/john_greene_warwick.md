## Identity

- **Full designation:** Major John Greene of Occupasuetuxet (also John Greene Jr. in early records — "Jr." relative to his father the Surgeon)
- **Born:** Baptized **15 August 1620**, St. Thomas's Church, Salisbury, Wiltshire, England
- **Died:** **27 November 1708**, Warwick, Rhode Island. Gravestone states "in his 89th year"
- **Father:** Surgeon John Greene (d. 1659) and Joane Tattershall of Salisbury
- **Wife:** **Ann/Anne Almy** (c. 1627 – 6 May 1709), daughter of William and Audrey Almy of Portsmouth. Married c. 1648

**Sources:** Baptismal record at St. Thomas's, Salisbury; gravestone inscription; *The Greenes of Rhode Island* (archive.org)

## Offices Held

- Member, Warwick's first Town Council — 8 August 1647 (named first of six)
- Representative/Commissioner from Warwick — 1652–1663
- General Recorder / Secretary of State — 1652–1654
- General Solicitor — 1655
- Attorney General — 1657–1660
- Warden for Warwick — 1658
- Assistant — c. 1660–1690
- Deputy — 1664, 1674, 1677, 1680
- Major for the Main (mainland militia) — 1683–1686, 1690–1691, 1696
- Member of Sir Edmund Andros's Council — appointed 16 April 1688
- **Deputy Governor of Rhode Island — 1690–1700**

**Source:** *Records of the Colony of RI*; published colonial office lists

## Key Land Records

- **Occupasuetuxet / Greene's Hold** (~700 acres): Originally purchased from Miantonomi by Surgeon John Greene on **1 October 1642**; inherited by Major John via his father's will (dated 28 Dec 1658, proved 7 Jan 1659), which gave him "that necke of land called Occuppesuatuxet," meadow, and adjoining island
- **Shawomet/Warwick Purchase** (12 January 1642): From Miantonomi by Randall Holden, John Greene, Samuel Gorton, and others
- Also held interests in Providence home lots, Coweset, Scituate, Warwick Neck, Grand Purchase, Natick, and Tunkhill

**Will:** Dated 20 December 1706; divided holdings among sons Peter, Job, Richard, and Samuel; provided life interest for Ann; named four sons as executors.

## What Distinguishes Him from the Other Johns

1. **English baptismal record** — Salisbury, Wiltshire (none for the other two)
2. **Wife Ann Almy** — not Joan, not Mary Jefferay
3. **11 children** — completely different list from Quidnessett children
4. **Warwick freeman** 1647 — not Newport, not Acquidnesset
5. **Deputy Governor** 1690–1700 — the Quidnessett John held no recorded colonial office
6. **Occupasuetuxet property** — inherited from the Surgeon; Quidnessett John's land was at Cocumscussoc/Devil's Foot
7. **Y-DNA:** Researcher's Kit B2778 (R-Z255 / Irish Sea cluster) does NOT match the expected English Greene modal from Salisbury. This is consistent with the Quidnessett line being a **different patriline** (see `research/ydna_summary.md`)
