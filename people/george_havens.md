## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 bottom (NK Land Records):** Seller in the deed where "Beni Green of Kingstown" pays thirty pounds.
- **Edward² deeds:** Edward also sold land to George Havens in 1695.
- **Connection:** Havens bought from at least TWO of John Sr.'s sons — Benjamin and Edward. This makes Havens a significant figure in the post-John-Sr. land network.
