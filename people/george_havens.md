# George Havens

**Status:** 🟡 SECONDARY — appears on Image 10 bottom and in Edward² deed references  
**Role in archive:** Land buyer connected to both Benjamin² and Edward² Greene.

---

## What We Know (sourced)

- **Image 10 bottom (NK Land Records):** Seller in the deed where "Beni Green of Kingstown" pays thirty pounds.
- **Edward² deeds:** Edward also sold land to George Havens in 1695.
- **Connection:** Havens bought from at least TWO of John Sr.'s sons — Benjamin and Edward. This makes Havens a significant figure in the post-John-Sr. land network.

## What We Do NOT Know

- ❌ His dates, family, and background
- ❌ Whether Havens knew Joan personally
- ❌ Whether the land Havens bought originally belonged to John Sr.'s estate
- ❌ Full text of the Havens ↔ Greene transactions

## How Havens Could Surface Joan

- **His deeds with the Greene sons.** If any of those deeds reference the original 1682 conveyance, the life-estate clause, or "the widow Greene," Joan appears.
- **His own probate.** If Havens's estate records mention land "formerly of John Greene" or obligations attached to Greene land, that's a lead.

## Where to Look

1. NK Land Records — "George Havens" in the grantor/grantee index
2. Full read of Image 10 bottom deed
3. NK probate records for Havens
