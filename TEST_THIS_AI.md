All records from this period were created within colonial legal systems and reflect their biases, categories, and blind spots. Every name found here — English, Narragansett, Niantic, mixed-heritage, unknown — represents a real person whose full story these records were not designed to capture. Content may include references to war, captivity, enslavement, displacement, legal coercion, and loss. These are documented realities of the period, not abstractions.

> # TEST THIS AI — Joan Archive Litmus Test

**Purpose:** A self-contained test that any AI can run against this repository to validate whether it can follow the archive's methodology chain and report facts honestly.

**Why this exists:** On 2026-09-06/07, the archive tested 17 AI tools against this repo. The results showed that AIs which followed the required reading order got the right answer; those that didn't, fabricated or drifted. This file makes the test reproducible without a human proctor.

## Answer Key (for human verification)

**Verified facts:** 4
1. Joan is named as wife of John Greene in a March 1682 land deed.
2. A life annuity is reserved to Joan, payable during her natural life. (Amount is an open question.)
3. Joan does not sign or mark the 1682 deed.
4. No other record of Joan has been located.

**Maiden name:** Unknown. "Tibbitts" is discredited. "Beggarly/Beggerly" is eliminated (La Mance 1904 error).

**Suspended items:** 8 (S-001 through S-008)
- S-001: Joan signed with a mark — no source says this for the March 1682 deed
- S-002: Joan gave consent — Worth doesn't mention consent; Bates is summary language
- S-003: Free and voluntary consent — phrase may belong to the suspended Pawtuxet deed
- S-004: Or to her mother if she survive — not in any source the archive holds
- S-005: Joan as co-grantor — Bates summary doesn't confirm legal role
- S-006: Joan as legal actor — derivative of S-005
- S-007: Pawtuxet deed (May 19, 1682) — citation invalidated
- S-008: Joan's death date (1682) — widely repeated but undocumented

**Resolution path:** S-001 through S-006 resolve via manuscript examination (FamilySearch DGS 008204949, images 9–12). S-007 requires locating the actual Pawtuxet deed. S-008 requires a primary death/burial/probate record.

## Scoring Guide

**Tier 1 — Full framework comprehension:** 4 facts correct, maiden name unknown, all 8 suspended items identified with accurate reasoning, methodology chain followed.

**Tier 2 — Strong partial:** 4 facts correct, maiden name unknown, some suspended items identified, partial methodology chain followed.
