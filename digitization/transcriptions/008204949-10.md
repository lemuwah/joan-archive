# Image 10 — John Green deed (Daniel instrument), March 1681/2

## AI Transcription Notice
Three independent AI reads (Claude once, Migoo twice from different resolution images). All are **draft, unverified readings only** per Law 4. The three reads converge on names and structure. The second Migoo pass used higher-resolution user-uploaded images and was able to partially read the **LEFT PAGE (deed body)** for the first time.

## Instrument Identification
**This is the Daniel instrument** — the recording entry for John Green's deed of 120 acres to his son Daniel Greene, dated March 24, 1681/82. Already identified in `primary_sources/homeplace-1682.md`.

The **James instrument** (60 acres to son James) is abstracted in Worth 1921 pp. 173–174. The Daniel instrument has NOT been abstracted by Worth — this manuscript image is its only known surviving text.

### C-3: Mother clause wording
**NOT RESOLVED.** The specific phrase "or to her mother if she survive" is NOT visible in Image 10 at any resolution attempted. What IS visible is a life-estate clause referencing "his Wife or during her natural life" — but the full construction is obscured by damage.

### Joan signed with a mark
**ADDRESSED.** The execution block (right page) shows ONLY John Green signing. No mark or signature for Joan. This is consistent with Joan being a beneficiary rather than a co-grantor — a beneficiary would not sign the deed.
