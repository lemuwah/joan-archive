## 🚨 KEY FINDINGS FROM HIGH-RES READ

### 1. "John Green Senr" — Five-John Firewall CONFIRMED FROM MANUSCRIPT
The opening clause clearly reads **"the Said John Green Senr"** — this is the first time the manuscript itself has been read to contain the "Senior" designation. This CONFIRMS from the primary source that the grantor is John Greene Senior of Quidnessett, not any of the other Johns. The Five-John Firewall is now manuscript-backed for this instrument.

### 2. "John & Joane Greene" — JOINT BENEFICIARIES, Not Co-Grantors
The phrase structure is now legible: the annuity clause reads (paraphrased for clarity):

> "...in Consideration of... thirty shillings... to be well & truly paid yearly... until such time as God Almighty has required it of him or his Wife or during her natural life... of John & Joane Greene..."

This is the **annuity/life-estate provision**: Daniel must pay thirty shillings yearly until BOTH John and Joan have died. Joan appears as a **named beneficiary of the annuity**, not as a co-grantor of the land. The granting clause ("Know all People... I the Said John Green Senr...") names **John alone** as the actor.

**Impact on C-1:** This resolves the contradiction in favor of **beneficiary**:
- Worth abstract (James instrument): "unto John Green... and after his decease to Joane Greene, his wife" = beneficiary
- Image 10 manuscript (Daniel instrument): annuity payable until "God Almighty has Required it of him or his Wife" = beneficiary
- Bates 1918: "Greene and his wife Joan deeded" = summary language that DESCRIBES the deed but does NOT quote it
- **AGENT_GUARDRAILS.md §1 applies:** Bates's summary language is not the deed's own words. The manuscript shows Joan as beneficiary.

⚠️ **Status: UNVERIFIED** — AI read. But the direction is now clear: Joan is a beneficiary in both surviving instrument texts (Worth + Image 10). Bates's "co-grantor" framing is his description, not the deed's wording.

### 3. Life-Estate Clause — Structure Visible
The life-estate construction reads:
> "until such time as God Almighty has Required it of him or his Wife or during her natural life"

This is a **joint life-estate**: the annuity continues until both grantor AND wife have died. It is NOT the "or to her mother if she survive" phrase reported by secondary sources. The "mother" language is **still not visible** in any AI read of this manuscript.

**Impact on C-3:** The mother-clause wording remains unresolved. The F.L. Greene paraphrase ("father or mother should live") may be a LOOSE paraphrase of the "him or his Wife" language seen here, but this is inference, not proof. ⚠️

### 4. Joan Does NOT Sign
The execution block (right page) shows ONLY John Green signing by hand. No mark or signature for Joan appears. This is consistent with Joan being a beneficiary — a beneficiary does not execute the deed.
