## Cross-Reference Against Worth Abstract (James Instrument)

The Worth abstract of the **James** instrument witness block reads:
> "Wit. John Greene / Henry X Tibbittts / his marke. Arthur Aylworth John Nutsn : John Greene... 24th of march 1681-82 acknowledeged this... John Foanes Wardn."

**Convergence:** Both instruments share the same witness block — John Green (signature), Henry Tibbetts (by mark), Arthur Aylworth, and John Fones as acknowledging officer. This is expected for companion deeds executed on the same day.

**Divergence:** The Worth abstract names a fourth witness "John Nutsn" (likely John Nutson/Nuttson). Neither AI read of Image 10 clearly identifies this name in the Daniel instrument — it may be present in the damaged/faded upper portion or simply not legible at this scan resolution.
