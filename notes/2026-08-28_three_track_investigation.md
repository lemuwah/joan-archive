## TRACK 1: GEOGRAPHIC — CRITICAL FINDING

**Nisquitianxsett/Misquamicutt = modern Westerly, RI — ~25 miles SOUTH of Quidnessett.**

The 1661 deed where Awashouse signs is for Misquamicutt (Westerly), NOT Quidnessett (North Kingstown).

| Place | Modern Location | Distance from Quidnessett |
|-------|----------------|---------------------------|
| Quidnessett | North Kingstown | — (home base) |
| Cocumscussoc/Wickford | North Kingstown | ~1 mi |
| Annaquatucket River | North Kingstown | ~1 mi south |
| Namcock Neck | Narragansett | ~5 mi south |
| Nisquitianxsett/Misquamicutt | Westerly | **~25-30 mi south** |

**What this means:** Awashouse signed a deed 25 miles from Quidnessett — but as one of EIGHT confirming sachems (alongside Cachaquant/Coginaquand, Pessicus, Wawaloam). This indicates **paramount-level authority across all Narragansett Country**, consistent with Anashuecot being "chiefe Sachem and heire properly" in 1672.

**Cachaquant = Coginaquand CONFIRMED** — both sign the 1661 deed, proving Awashous and Cachaquant are DIFFERENT PEOPLE.

**Hostile note:** Shadow Model A (different sachems) slightly strengthened by geographic distance. But council-level confirmation explains the signature without requiring local authority.

## TRACK 2: SCRIBE ATTRIBUTION — H7 STRENGTHENED

**John Fones** served as recorder ~1686–1703. The 1672 deed was executed 1672 but Fones enrolled it **1703** — 31 years later.

| Variant Cluster | Scribe Context | Documents |
|----------------|----------------|----------|
| **An-** (Anashuecot, Anashuecut, etc.) | Fones's hand, 1703 copying session | 1672 deed, 1677/78 affidavit |
| **Aw-** (Awashous, Awashouse, etc.) | Multiple non-Fones clerks, 1660s originals | 1661 Misquamicutt deed, Potter 1835 sources |

**Key insight:** The An- cluster may represent FONES'S 1703 rendering of a name originally written Aw- in the 1672 original. If so, the "original" spelling may have been closer to **Awashuecot** — bridging both clusters.

**Test:** Locating the 1672 original manuscript at RIHS (GAP-1) and comparing spelling against Fones's copy would RESOLVE this definitively.
