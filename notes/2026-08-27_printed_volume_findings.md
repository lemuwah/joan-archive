## Findings

### 1. Joan Greene's 1682 Deed — CONFIRMED IN VOLUME

The volume contains an abstracted version of the 24 March 1681/82 deed:
- **Grantor:** John Greene of Aquidnesett, in the Narragansett Country
- **Beneficiary:** "Joane Greene, his wife" — thirty shillings yearly
- **Grantee:** James Greene, described as "natural son of John and Joan Greene"
- **Terms:** Each son to pay thirty shillings yearly while either parent lived; land bordered Allen's Harbor and land of son John

**Exact page number:** NOT DETERMINED from the OCR text. The OCR does not preserve page breaks cleanly. A manual check of the Internet Archive page-image viewer would be needed to determine the exact page.

**Classification:** CONFIRMED — deed is in this volume. Page number needs manual lookup.

### 2. Henry Tibbitts — "his marke" — NEW CONNECTION

The OCR text shows **"Henry Tibbitts, his marke"** as a witness on a document dated **24 March 1681–82** — the SAME DATE as Joan's home-place deed.

**Why this matters:**
- Henry Tibbitts was the husband of **Mary Tibbitts** (née Greene?), one of the comparator women in Joan's social orbit
- The Tibbitts family was connected to the Greene family across multiple transactions
- Henry Tibbitts also signed with his mark (not a signature), meaning he was non-literate like Joan
- His presence as a witness on the same date as Joan's deed places him in the same room, same day
- **This is social network evidence** — it connects Joan to the Tibbitts family through physical co-presence at a legal event

**Warning (Law 1):** We cannot infer the nature of the Tibbitts-Greene relationship from this alone. Co-presence at a deed signing could mean family, neighbor, church member, or simply "available male witness." The connection is documented but not yet interpreted.

**Classification:** DOCUMENTED FACT — Henry Tibbitts witnessed a March 1681/82 document with his mark.
