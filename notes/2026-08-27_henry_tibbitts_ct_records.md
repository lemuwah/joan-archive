## Summary

Henry Tibbitts appears in **both** the 1663 Narragansett jurisdiction petition (Vol. 1) and multiple Vol. 2 entries as a Wickford constable under Connecticut authority. He was also seized by Connecticut, generating a Rhode Island protest. This confirms he was politically active in the Narragansett disputes, not just a geographic neighbor.

## Appearances

1. **July 3, 1663:** Signed the Narragansett jurisdiction petition alongside John Greene and Richard Smith (Vol. 1)
2. **Vol. 2, p. 138:** Appointed constable of Wickford with Samuel Eldred
3. **Vol. 2, p. 157:** Re-appointed constable for Wickford
4. **Vol. 2, p. 540:** Named in Rhode Island complaint — seized by Connecticut authority along with Thomas Gould and James Reynolds
5. **Vol. 2, p. 553:** Listed among Wickford constables
