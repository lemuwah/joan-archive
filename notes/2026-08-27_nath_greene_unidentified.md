## The Record

**Nath. Greene** witnessed the Coginaquand/Namcock Neck deed (recorded July 4, 1687). Other witnesses: John Vial, Thomas Stanton, John Stanton, John Farniside.

## Identity: Unresolved

**Nath. Greene is NOT identified as a son of John Greene Sr. of Quidnessett.** The known children from Bates (1918–19) are:
- Lieut. John²
- Henry²
- Daniel²
- James²
- Benjamin²
- Sarah²
- Edward² (disputed/absent from Bates's primary sibling list but treated as heir elsewhere)

**Nathaniel does not appear on any established list of John Sr. and Joan's children.**

## Possible Identifications

1. **Warwick Greene line** — More plausible. The Warwick Greenes (descended from Surgeon John Greene of Warwick, a different person per the Five-John Firewall) include Nathaniel as a documented name. However, the specific Warwick Nathaniel known from records was born c.1679 (son of Thomas Greene), making him approximately 8 years old in 1687 — too young to be an adult witness.
2. **Unknown Quidnessett Greene** — Less plausible. Would require adding a child to the established list without evidence.
3. **Unrelated Greene** — Possible. Greene was a common surname in colonial Rhode Island.

**Per Law 3 (No Premature Disqualification):** No identification is forced. Nath. Greene remains an unresolved name.

## Five-John Firewall Status

**Intact.** This finding does not merge Warwick and Quidnessett Greenes. It documents a witness whose family line is unresolved and flags the need to distinguish Greene lines in every document.
