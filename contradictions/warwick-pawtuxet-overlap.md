Origin: challenge to the Five-John Firewall — is Quidnessett John actually

## ⚠️ CONTRADICTIONS / OPEN — not resolved either direction

### C1. Pawtuxet overlap (NEW — upgrade from coincidence to documented link)
The 1682 Pawtuxet deed (John & Joan Greene → William Carpenter, "inherited
Pawtuxet land") is witnessed by **John Wickes and Samuel Gorton** — both
original 1643 Shawomet purchasers. Separately, Surgeon John Greene is
documented purchasing land at Pawtuxet itself in 1641/2, adjoining
Occupessatuxet Cove, before the Shawomet purchase.
**Status:** Real, primary-sourced network overlap between the Quidnessett
and Warwick/Shawomet spheres. Does NOT by itself prove shared identity —
cousins with witnessed shared land history is a normal explanation — but
the Firewall table's implicit "these spheres don't touch" framing is now
contradicted by a specific document. Needs its own entry, not smoothed
into either the Firewall table or dismissed.
**Next step:** Pull the full 1682 Pawtuxet deed text — does "inherited"
specify inherited *from whom*? That single word could resolve this.

### C2. Providence/Smith concurrency (open, not disproven)
Claim: "40 years and more with Smith" and active Shawomet-circle membership
in Providence/Pawtuxet (1641–43) could both be true if the same man moved
between the two.
**Against:** No document places Quidnessett John in Providence/Pawtuxet at
any point 1637–1643. The self-testimony (arrived w/ Smith at Narragansett
"before 1639") is the only first-person location evidence for this window.
**For:** Geographic distance (~15–18 mi) doesn't make travel impossible.
**Status:** Logged open. Absence of a document ≠ evidence for the
alternate location. Needs a positive document (not just non-contradiction)
to move this to Confirmed.

### C3. "Filled Gorton's shoes as Crown correspondent"
Later (1670s–80s) petitions/certificates show Quidnessett John active in
RI civic/political affairs, parallel in *function* to Gorton's earlier
Crown appeals.
**Problem:** This reasons backward from a later role to an unproven earlier
partnership. RI political competency was learnable many ways (decades of
his own civic involvement, kinship with the Warwick Greenes, general RI
political culture) without requiring direct 1642–43 partnership with Gorton.
**Status:** Speculative inference, not evidence. Logged open.

### C4. Unknown birthdate for son "Jr" (Edward)
No baptismal record found for Edward or any child. The ~1643 birth estimate
comes from secondary reasoning (e.g., grandson's age in 1695), not a
primary vital record.
**Status:** Confirmed as a genuine gap — this weakens (does not eliminate)
prior arguments built on assuming Edward was an infant in 1643. Log as
unknown, not assumed.

### C5. Father also named "John"? (tested, low support)
Hypothesis: the "John Greene, and his son John" arrested in 1643 could be
Quidnessett John's own father + himself, both named John.
**Against:** Every compiled source names his father as Robert Greene
(b. 1580, Gillingham) — not John. The "John Greene the Younger" epithet
traces to an unrelated 15th-century fugitive-regicide legend (a John
Greene b. ~1460), not a contemporary New England father.
**Caveat:** WikiTree flags his parentage as actively disputed — the door
isn't fully shut, just currently unsupported by anything found.
**Status:** Logged open, low support, needs a specific counter-document to
advance.

### C6. DNA haplogroup claim (real project, unverified specific result)
An active FamilyTreeDNA Green/Greene project exists and treats Quidnessett-
line and Warwick-line descendants as separate cohorts. Forum discussion
among project participants frames Y-DNA comparison as the intended method
to determine shared/unshared common ancestry.
**Not yet verified:** The specific claim that confirmed Quidnessett-line
haplogroups differ entirely from confirmed Warwick-line haplogroups. One
Quidnessett-descendant tester's haplogroup is listed as **R-BY186325**
(WikiTree, kit #B819637) — no comparable confirmed Surgeon-line haplogroup
was found in this session's searches.
**Status:** HIGHEST-PRIORITY item to source. If you have the FTDNA
kit/haplogroup for a *documented* Surgeon-line patrilineal descendant,
log it next to R-BY186325 here and this resolves at primary-source rigor.

## ✅ CONFIRMED — new, sourced, usable today

### F1. "John Greene" as a common period alias
Austin, *Genealogical Dictionary of Rhode Island* (1887), per Caskey
compilation: c.1639, three unrelated men all named "John Greene" appeared
in the colony, likely men "wanted as heretics back in England" — the name
functioned as the era's version of "John Smith" for anonymity.
**Implication:** Cuts both ways — argues for *more* caution before merging
any two John Greene references, not less. Include in methodology notes as
a reason the Firewall needs to stay strict, not loosen.

### F2. "John Greene the Younger" origin traced
The epithet does not indicate a contemporary father in the colony. It
traces to an unrelated, much older family legend (a fugitive-regicide
story misattached to a John Greene b. ~1460, Gillingham). Rules out C5 as
currently framed, pending any new counter-document.

### F3. Pawtuxet as a real, shared place-name (not coincidence)
Confirmed as one specific tract at the mouth of the Pawtuxet River,
directly north of/adjoining the Shawomet purchase — not a phonetic false
cluster. (See C1 for the evidentiary weight this carries.)
