> This archive documents lives lived between approximately 1600–1750 in Narragansett country (present-day Rhode Island). All records from this period were created within colonial legal systems and reflect their biases, categories, and blind spots. Every name found here — English, Narragansett, Niantic, mixed-heritage, unknown — represents a real person whose full story these records were not designed to capture. Content may include references to war, captivity, enslavement, displacement, legal coercion, and loss. These are documented realities of the period, not abstractions. All cultural and genealogical content is included for research purposes in the quest to identify Joan Unknown Greene and will be revised or removed if requested by descendant communities or tribal representatives.

## How This Works

1. Every discrete claim gets its own row. "Joan was John's wife" is one claim. "Joan signed with a mark" is a separate claim.
2. Every claim enters as ⬜ **UNTESTED** regardless of what the source file previously said. The spine does not inherit opinions from the old system. It tests them fresh.
3. Claims are tested against Laws 1–7. When claims converge on an identity, the convergence is tested via Law 8 (The Recursive Pass).
4. Nothing is deleted. Killed claims stay and show the kill. Resurrected claims show the resurrection.
5. The system generates recommended tests — archives to check, documents to find, alternatives to rule out.

## Status Values

- ⬜ **UNTESTED** — claim entered the spine but hasn't been run through the laws yet
- 🟢 **TESTED — SURVIVES** — ran through Laws 1–7, no flags, evidence chain intact
- 🟡 **TESTED — FLAGGED** — ran through the laws, one or more flags remain (specific law and resolving test documented)
- 🔴 **TESTED — KILLED** — ran through the laws, claim does not survive (specific law and kill reason documented). Never deleted.
- 🔵 **UNTESTABLE** — no known archive or surviving record that could confirm or deny. Not false, not confirmed. Parked.
- 🟠 **CONVERGENCE PENDING** — this claim is the product of converging edges; Law 8 recursive pass not yet complete
