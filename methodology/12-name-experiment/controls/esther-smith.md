> This archive documents lives lived between approximately 1600–1750 in Narragansett country (present-day Rhode Island). All records from this period were created within colonial legal systems and reflect their biases, categories, and blind spots. Every name found here — English, Narragansett, Niantic, mixed-heritage, unknown — represents a real person whose full story these records were not designed to capture. Content may include references to war, captivity, enslavement, displacement, legal coercion, and loss. These are documented realities of the period, not abstractions. All cultural and genealogical content is included for research purposes in the quest to identify Joan Unknown Greene and will be revised or removed if requested by descendant communities or tribal representatives.

# Control Case: Esther Smith (wife of Richard Smith Jr.)

**Purpose:** Known identity used as the baseline control for the 12-name experiment. If the methodology cannot reconstruct Esther from the corpus alone, it cannot be trusted for unknowns.

---

## Known Network Signature

- **Name:** Esther (no maiden surname proven)
- **Husband:** Major Richard Smith Jr. of Wickford/Narragansett
- **Location:** Cocumscussoc (Smith's Castle), Narragansett country
- **Marriage:** ~1655 (no surviving civil or parish record — date is secondary inference)
- **Children:** None surviving (confirmed by will)
- **Will:** 1690/91, probated Boston, Suffolk County, 12 July 1692
- **Estate:** Lifetime possession; remainder to sister Elizabeth (Viall/Newman), nephews/nieces
- **Kinship language:** Richard called her his "cousin"; she sent love to Richard's "unkell William Barton" of Morton, Gloucestershire
- **Correspondence:** Personally wrote to John Winthrop Jr. (Winthrop Papers)
- **Barton connection:** See research_queue/esther-smith-barton-network.md

## What Makes This a Good Control

1. Her name alone ("Esther Smith") is not unique — multiple Esther Smiths exist in 17th-century New England
2. Her network signature IS unique — husband + property + location + kinship language + correspondence
3. She has a known contamination history — false surname insertions (Mount, Fosdick, Updike, Long Island conflation) have been documented and killed
4. She sits in the same geographic/temporal space as Joan Greene — neighboring Narragansett households

## The Test

Can the 12-name methodology reconstruct Esther's unique identity from the corpus data alone, without relying on secondary genealogy?

If yes → the methodology works for known identities and can be cautiously applied to unknowns.
If no → the methodology needs revision before applying it to Joan or anyone else.
