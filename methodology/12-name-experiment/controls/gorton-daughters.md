> This archive documents lives lived between approximately 1600–1750 in Narragansett country (present-day Rhode Island). All records from this period were created within colonial legal systems and reflect their biases, categories, and blind spots. Every name found here — English, Narragansett, Niantic, mixed-heritage, unknown — represents a real person whose full story these records were not designed to capture. Content may include references to war, captivity, enslavement, displacement, legal coercion, and loss. These are documented realities of the period, not abstractions. All cultural and genealogical content is included for research purposes in the quest to identify Joan Unknown Greene and will be revised or removed if requested by descendant communities or tribal representatives.

# Control Case: The Gorton Daughters (Samuel Gorton's 1677 Deed)

**Purpose:** Six related women in one document with full relational density — sisters, husbands, father, date, land — showing what maximum achievable resolution looks like structurally.

**Source:** Samuel Gorton deed of gift, 27 November 1677

**Why this is a control, not data:** Every one of these women has a fully known maiden name (Gorton). None of them are "unresolved" cases. Putting them in the data CSVs would inflate the "resolved" rate with women who were never actually unknown.

---

## The Six Named Daughters

Deed language (period wording preserved from source):

1. **Mary Gorton** → "unto my sonn John Sanford and my Daughter, Mary Sanford one Sixth part"
   - Husband: John Sanford
   - Note: Mary Gorton had TWO marriages: first to Peter Greene of Warwick, second to John Sanford. This is a different Mary Sanford than William Brenton's daughter (who married Peleg Sanford). **This collision was caught independently by the 12-name experiment's identity firewall — confirming the method works on known cases.**

2. **Sarah Gorton** → "unto my sonn William Maze and my Daughter Sarah Maze"
   - Husband: William Maze

3. **Anna Gorton** → "unto my sonn John Warner, and my Daughter Anna Warner"
   - Husband: John Warner

4. **Elizabeth Gorton** → "unto my sonn John Crandall and my Daughter Elizabeth Crandell"
   - Husband: John Crandall

5. **Susannah Gorton** → "unto my sonn Benjamin Barton and to my daughter Susanah Barton"
   - Husband: Benjamin Barton
   - Note: Susannah is outside the current 12-name list. Logged as overflow name in README.

6. **Mahershalalhashbaz Gorton** → paired with Cole (husband)
   - Husband: Cole (given name to be confirmed)
   - Note: Present in deed but unnamed by given name in some abstracts. Original transcript flagged correctly as present.

## What This Control Tests

- **Maximum relational density:** Father, husband, sisters, date, land — all in one document. This is the ceiling for identity resolution.
- **Collision detection:** The Mary Sanford / Mary Sanford collision (Gorton vs Brenton daughters) was caught by the identity firewall. Two different women, two different Sanford marriages, independently verified.
- **Variant tracking:** Anna (not Ann or Anne) appears as the deed spelling. Whether this is a distinct name or a variant is testable against the ann.csv and anne.csv populations.
