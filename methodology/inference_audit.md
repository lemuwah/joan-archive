## Purpose

This file catalogs claims currently on the site or in the repo that **feel** documented but are actually inferences from surrounding evidence. Each item must be either:
- **sourced** to a specific primary document → can stay, tagged 🟢 VERIFIED
- **moved** to `analysis.html` with proper status tags
- **rewritten** as two separate factual statements without the connecting inference
- **suspended** if the source support was withdrawn

## 🔴 Claims That Need a Primary Citation or Must Move to analysis.html

### 1. "John Greene's homestead survived King Philip's War"

**What was said:** The homestead was not destroyed during King Philip's War.  
**Why it's inference:** He partitions the same land in 1682. No source says the house stood.  
**Fix:** State the before/after documents. Leave the house status unsaid.  
**Correct wording:** "John Greene partitioned 180 acres at Quidnessett in 1682. His 1679 affidavit states he lived in Narragansett Country 'forty years and more.' The neighboring Cocumscussoc trading post was burned during King Philip's War (1676). No primary source confirms or denies damage to his homestead."

### 2. "Joan's single appearance is anomalous for an English woman"

**What was said:** Comparable wives appear more often in records.  
**Why it's inference:** No study establishes the baseline for Quidnessett wives.  
**Fix:** On `analysis.html`, present the comparison openly. "Mary Jefferay appears in X records. Joan appears in one. The baseline for Quidnessett wives has not been systematically compiled." That's Thread #16 (life-estate deed baseline).

### 3. "The Fones deed sat unrecorded for 31 years — why the delay?"

**What was said:** The delay is suspicious or significant.  
**Why it's inference:** The delay is documented fact (1672 execution, 1703 enrollment). But "delay" implies it should have been recorded sooner.  
**Fix:** State both dates. Note that Fones also entered other deeds at the same time. Whether 31 years is unusual requires a baseline of Fones's other recording intervals — a testable question, not a settled one.

### 4. "Joan's invisibility matches John's invisibility"

**What was said:** Neither has pre-Rhode Island documentation.  
**Why it's inference:** Pattern-matching across two absences.  
**Fix:** State both facts separately. "No pre-Rhode Island record has been located for John Greene of Quidnessett. No record of any kind has been located for Joan outside the 1682 deed." Let the reader see the pattern.

### 5. "Awashouse and Anashuecot are the same person"

**What was said:** Identity asserted or implied from name similarity.  
**Why it's inference:** Phonetic similarity + same region + same era. Potter uses "Awashowat" as a heading for the 1672 deed. Strong but not explicit.  
**Fix:** Present the variant table. Flag Potter's equation (Awashowat = 1672 grantor) as the strongest link. Note the An-/Aw- split as scribal convention. Don't assert identity as fact.  
**Status:** 🟡 PROBABLE — strong but not explicit.

### 6. "Tacommanan = Tocomminon"

**What was said:** Same person referenced by two variant spellings.  
**Why it's inference:** Same territory, same generation, same role, similar name.  
**Fix:** Present both names with their source documents side by side. Flag the probable match. Don't merge them in the timeline.  
**Status:** 🟡 PROBABLE

### 7. "John Greene had a direct personal relationship with Potuck"

**What was said:** Greene was personally connected to the sachem Potuck.  
**Why it's inference:** The document says he transported Potuck in his sloop. Transportation ≠ personal relationship.  
**Fix:** "John Greene transported the sachem Potuck by sloop for peace negotiations (1676). Potuck served as counselor to Quaiapin (Chapin 1931)." The transport is fact. "Relationship" is inference.

### 8. "The mother clause is anomalous"

**What was said:** The annuity's reversion to Joan's mother is unusual for colonial deeds.  
**Why it's inference:** No baseline of comparable deeds has been compiled. Thread #16 exists to test this.  
**Fix:** Present the clause wording (once manuscript is read). Note the absence of a baseline. The question is open.

### 9. "Cocumscussoc burned" (specific to Smith's trading post)

**What was said:** Widely stated across the archive.  
**Why it's inference:** Not inference per se — this IS documented in multiple published histories. But the archive should cite the specific primary source, not assume it.  
**Fix:** Find and cite the primary source before the rebuild. Almost certainly documentable but citation needed.

### 10. "John Greene served as Conservator of the Peace"

**What was said:** His title was Conservator.  
**Why it's inference:** His 1679 affidavit says "forty years and more" at Cocumscussoc. Does it specifically say Conservator? Or is that from Bates?  
**Fix:** Check the exact title in the primary affidavit vs Bates's characterization.

## 🟡 Borderline Items — Fact but Imprecise Language

### 11. "Joan comes from the surviving line"

**What was said:** Joan's origin connected to the surviving sachem line.  
**Why it's imprecise:** This is thesis language, not a fact. Joan's origin is unknown. Anashuecot's father's line survived the war. These are two separate statements unless Model A is proven.  
**Fix:** Never state this on `index.html`. On `analysis.html`, present it as what Hypothesis A requires.

### 12. "Two sachem lines, one territory"

**What was said:** The Narragansett political structure was two lines in one territory.  
**Why it's imprecise:** Simplification. The Narragansett political structure was more complex.  
**Fix:** Present what the documents show — Chapin's paramount succession AND the Tocomminon/Coheassuck entries in Potter — without asserting a neat two-line model. Tag as 📖 EXTERNAL INTERPRETATION.

### 13. "John Greene was embedded in Narragansett governance"

**What was said:** Greene was deeply integrated into Narragansett political systems.  
**Why it's imprecise:** Inference from: transport, oath, magistrate role, proximity. Each piece is documented. "Embedded" is a characterization.  
**Fix:** List the documented interactions. Let the pattern speak. "Embedded" does not go on a public page.

## How to Use This in the Rebuild

**index.html** — every claim must pass: *can I point to one specific document that says this?* If not, it moves to `analysis.html` or gets rewritten as two factual statements without the connecting inference.

**context.html** — colonial and Narragansett setting. Same test. Colonial statutes: cite them. Sachem succession: cite Chapin, cite Potter, cite the Fones Record. Don't synthesize across them.

**analysis.html** — where inferences live, clearly labeled. "The records show X. The records show Y. One explanation is Z. Another is W. Neither is proven."
