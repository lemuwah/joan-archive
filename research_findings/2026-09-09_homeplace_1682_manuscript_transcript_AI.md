## Folio 259 (left page)

**Folio number:** `259` (centered, top margin).

The page carries two entries: (a) the tail of a release, John Williams → Thomas
Terrey; then (b) the **commencement of the John Greene → James Greene deed**.

### (a) Release — John Williams to Thomas Terrey (preceding entry)

> Know all men by these Presents, that I John Williams of Boston in the County of
> Suffolke in New England Merch[t]: Doe acquitt and discharge Thomas Terrey of
> Block Island alias New-Shorrum of all debts bills bonds obligations Reconings
> acco[ts]: that I now have or forever hereafter shall have against him the said
> Tho[mas] Terrey his Heires Executors Administrators and assignes, (as in Relation
> to my Owne perticular Concern[e]) from the begining of the world to the day of
> the date of these Presents. In Witness whereof I have hereunto sett my hand this
> 29th of August 1677: I doe alsoe discharge the said Terrey as Attorny to Capt[n]
> Williams from the debt of William Harris
>
> Signed and Delivered — Jn[o] Williams
> In Presenses off:
>   his
> John I R Rathbone — A True Copy of the Originall Entred and Recorded
>   marke — p[er] John Sanford Recorder:
> George Halsall

### (b) Deed — John Greene to James Greene (commencement)

**Marginal note (left margin):**
> Deed of Gift or Sale — John Green[e] to James Greene

> To all Christian People unto whome these Presents shall Come
> [interlined/struck: Deed of Gift or Sale shall come], John Greene of Aquidnesett
> in the Kings Province in the Narragansett Cuntry in New England Send Greeting:
> Know yee that the said John Greene, for Divers good Causes and affectioned
> Considerations at this Present espetially moveing, and more perticularly for and
> in Consideration of the full Sum of **thirtie Shillings in Currant mony of New
> England to be well and truly paid Yearly and every yeare from and after the day
> of the date of these Presents, each yeare thirty Shillings as aforesaid, unto the
> aforesaid John Greene Dureing his naturall life and after his decease, to Joane
> Greene his wife for and Dureing her naturall life By James Greene (the naturall
> son of John and Joan Greene aforesaid) his heires Executors Administrators or
> assignes** Have given granted Barganied, Aliened Infeoffed and Confirmed, and by
> these presents Doe fully Cleerly and absolutly give, grant, bargaine, alien,
> enfeoff and Confirme, unto the afores[ai]d James Greene his heires Executors
> Administrato[rs] and assignes all that his tract or percell of Land Scittuate
> lying and beinge at Aquednessett aforesaid, and now in posesion of the aforesaid
> James Greene Containeinge by Esteemation **Sixty Acres** more or less being
> butted and bounded as followeth (viz) **Northerly by the land of the said John
> Greene Easterly by Quidnesett Harbo[ur] Southerly by a River or brook and land
> of Daniell Greene, and Westerly by a hie way**; To have and to hold the said land
> and every part thereof, with all and Singular the wood, underwood Timber Stone
> Quarries waters water courses buildings fencesings [struck/interlined: Easments]
> Rights proffitts Previledges Jmuneties Hereditaments appurtenances on the same
> belonging or to it or to any part or percel thereof belonging or in any wise
> appertaininge, unto the said James Greene his heires Executors Administrators and
> assignes forever. To the Only Proper use Benefitt and behoofe of him the said
> James Greene his heires and assignes forever, And the said John Greene Doth
> hereby Covenant and promise to and with the said James Greene his heires
> Executors Administrators and assign[es] that at the Insealing and Delivery of
> these Presents, he was the true Sol[e] and Lawfull owner of all and every part
> of the above granted Premises, and hath in himselfe full Power good Right and
> Lawfull Authorety to give grant Sell, assure and Convey the same unto the said
> James Greene his heires and assignes for ever, as a good perfect and absolute
> Estate of Inheritance in fee Simple, without any manner of Condition, Reversion
> or Limitation whatsoever, Only what is herein above Expressed: Soe as to alter —
> [continues on folio 260]

## Paleographer's notes (AI) — flags for human review

**Joan / Joane — every reference:**
- Folio 259, consideration clause: "...after his decease, to **Joane Greene his
  wife** for and Dureing her naturall life By James Greene (the naturall son of
  **John and Joan Greene** aforesaid)..."
- Folio 260, proviso: "Duering the naturall lives of the above sd John Greene and
  **Joane his wife**..." and "at the decease of the said John and **Joane
  Greene**..."

**Matrilineal reverter / "or to her mother if she survive" clause: ABSENT.**
The proviso runs the annuity during the natural lives of John and Joane and
ceases at their deaths, leaving the estate wholly free in James. No reverter to
any mother. (This is the AI read; pending human confirmation — see
`contradictions/mother-clause-wording.md`.)

**Joan's signature / mark: NONE.** Joan is named only within the body text as
wife and life-annuity beneficiary. She does not sign, make a mark, or appear as an
executant. The only mark on the instrument is **Henry Tibbitts's** — a capital
letter **"H"** (not a pictograph, not a bow-and-arrow). The bow-and-arrow mark
tradition associated with Anashuecot is therefore **not present on this
instrument**; it belongs to a separate attestation (see the Absolom affidavit /
Anashuecot threads), not to this deed.

**Other hands / authorities:**
- Grantor John Greene signs "John Greene" with a pen-drawn seal `(S)` (locus
  sigilli).
- Witnesses: Henry H Tibbitts (mark "H"); Arthur Aylworth (signature); John Nuton
  (signature).
- Acknowledged before **John Fones, Wardn** (Warden), 24 March 1681/82.
- Recorded by **John Sanford, Recorder** ("A True Copy of the Originall Entred
  and Recorded p[er] John Sanford Recorder").
- Note: Worth 1921 and F.L. Greene give John Fones as the recording authority;
  the manuscript distinguishes Fones = acknowledging warden, Sanford = recorder
  who entered the copy.

**Scribal quirks / uncertain tokens:**
- Folio 259 header: scribe first penned a running title "Deed of Gift or Sale
  shall come" after the salutation, then crossed/interlined it.
- Folio 259: "fencesings" followed by a deleted "Easments" before "Rights."
- Folio 259: "Quidnesett Harbo[ur]" — "ur" as superscript flourish near gutter.
- Folio 260: "Concerni[n]ge" with titulus/suspension; "aganist" (phonetic);
  "laymg" (minim error, = laying); "Jmuneties" (= immunities).
- Folio 260: "Premises" struck through before "above-granted premises."
- Folio 260: contemporary attestation of two interlineations made before sealing
  — "(lawfull)" in the 21st line and "(the sd John Greene his)" in the 23d.
- Bleed-through from the verso is visible on both folios (middle third / lower
  folio 260) but does not obscure the primary ink.

## What the transcript settles (for the Synthesizer, pending review)
1. **Verbatim life-annuity clause** now in hand from the original: "after his
   decease, to Joane Greene his wife for and Dureing her naturall life."
2. **C-1 (Joan's role):** beneficiary only; not a signatory, not a co-grantor.
3. **C-3 ("or to her mother if she survive"):** not present in this instrument.
4. **Mark analysis:** no bow-and-arrow mark on this deed; only Tibbitts's "H."
5. **Boundaries confirmed:** 60 ac at Aquednessett; N by John Greene; E by
   Quidnessett harbour; S by a river/brook & Daniell Greene; W by a highway.
