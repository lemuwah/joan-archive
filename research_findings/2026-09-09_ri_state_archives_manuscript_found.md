## What was found

The **original 17th-century manuscript** of the 24 March 1681/82 home-place
deed — the instrument that names **Joan Greene** — is held and fully digitized by
the **Rhode Island State Archives Digital Archive (Preservica)**, in
*Land Records No. 1, 1648–1696*, **folios 259–260** (image `_133`).

This is the exact volume Worthington abstracted in 1921 (RILE-I). The archive
had never seen the manuscript itself; `digital_access_map.md` flagged the RI
State Digital Archives as **🟡 UNREAD** and `CORRECTIONS.md` called its
digitization "the single most valuable digitization ask." It is now read.

## What the manuscript says (🟡 AI-READ, pending paleography)

**Grantor:** John Greene of Aquednesett in the King's Province in the
Narragansett Country. **Grantee:** James Greene (his natural son). **Date:**
24 March 1681/2 (34th year of Charles II). **Property:** 60 acres at
Aquednesett. **Marginal note:** "Deed of Gift &c: John Greene to James Greene."

**Boundaries:** Northerly by the land of John Greene; Easterly by Quidnesset
harbour; Southerly by a River or brook and land of Daniell Greene; Westerly by a
highway.

**Consideration / life-annuity clause (verbatim AI read):**
> "...for and in consideration of the full sum of thirtie shillings in Curent
> mony of New England to be well and truly paid yearly and every yeare from and
> after the day of the date of these Presents, each yeare thirty shillings as
> afore said, **unto the afore said John Greene Duringe his naturall life and
> after his decease to Joane Greene his wife for and Dureing her naturall life**
> By James Greene (the Naturall son of John and Joan Greene aforesaid) his heires
> Executors Administrators or assignes..."

**Proviso (verbatim AI read):**
> "Provided alwaies that the said thirty Shillinge as afore said be truly and
> duly paid yearly and every yeare Dureing the naturall lives of the above sd
> John Greene and Joane his wife as is above Expresed, by the sd James Greene...
> And at the decease of the Said John and Joane Greene to be free and freely
> acquitted and discharged..."

**Signatures / marks:** John Greene signs `John Greene` with a round seal.
**Joan Greene does NOT sign and makes NO mark** — she is named only within the
body text as wife and life-annuity beneficiary, not as an executing party.
Witnesses: `Henry H Tibbitts his marke` (mark = capital `H`);
`Arthur Aylwoth` (signature); `John Nuton` (signature). Acknowledged before
`John Fones Wardn` on 24 March 1681/82; recorded by `John Sanford Recorder`.

## What this changes / confirms (for the Synthesizer)

1. **Proof-level manuscript now in hand** for the one document that names Joan
   — upgrading the James instrument from Worth's 🟡 PLAUSIBLE abstract to a
   located, citable 🟢 manuscript (transcription still 🟡 pending paleography).
2. **C-1 (Joan's role): confirmed beneficiary, not signatory.** Joan is the
   life-annuity beneficiary ("after his decease to Joane Greene his wife for
   and Dureing her naturall life"). She does not sign or mark. The "co-grantor"
   reading is not supported by the manuscript.
3. **C-3 (the "or to her mother if she survive" clause): NOT in the manuscript.**
   The manuscript gives the annuity to Joan "for and Dureing her naturall life"
   with no matrilineal reverter. The "or to her mother if she survive" wording
   is absent from the primary source (as `contradictions/mother-clause-wording.md`
   already suspected). Pending paleographer confirmation.
4. **Recording clerk:** the colony manuscript records the acknowledgment before
   **John Fones (Warden)** and the entry by **John Sanford (Recorder)**. Worth
   and F.L. Greene give John Fones as the recording authority; the manuscript
   clarifies Fones = acknowledging warden, Sanford = recorder.
5. **The "page 173 = Joan's deed" note in `digital_access_map.md` is a
   conflation** (Worth's printed page ↔ manuscript folio). Correct folio is
   259–260. The archive should correct this so future agents don't fetch folio
   173 (which is an unrelated 1684–88 deed cluster).
6. **Daniel instrument (120 ac) is NOT in this colony volume** — Worth abstracts
   only the James (60 ac) deed from RILE-I. The Daniel instrument remains in the
   NK town records (DGS 008204949, Image 10), consistent with `joan_priority_search.md`.
7. **The modern "Name Index" personal-name entry "Joan ux/Henry [Brightman]
   p.173" is almost certainly a misindexing.** The manuscript's own folio index
   and the manuscript itself show Joan = wife of **John** Greene at 259–260; the
   "Henry Brightman" deed (George Sisson → Henry Brightman, 3 March 1681/2) at
   folios 257–258 names no Joan. Visual confirmation of that index line is still
   warranted, but no second Joan is supported.
