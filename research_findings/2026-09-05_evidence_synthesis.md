### The Succession Question

**Between 1660 and 1672:**
- Scuttup dies (1668)
- Quequaquenuit dies (pre-1665)
- Quaiapin takes paramount authority with Potuck and Quinnapin
- Anashuecot appears as "Chiefe Sachem" of Quidnessett specifically

**The Absolom affidavit says:** Anashuecot's father = **Tocomminion**
**Potter 1835 says:** **Tacommanan** submitted Coheassuck lands in 1659, with his grandchild **Namowish**

**The comparison:**

| Feature | Tocomminion (Absolom) | Tacommanan (Potter) |
|---------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Phonetic match | Tocomminion | Tacommanan |
| Active period | Pre-1677/8 | 1659 |
| Territory | Narragansett/Quidnessett | Coheassuck (adjacent) |
| Named descendants | Anashuecot (child), "James" and "Washaucuct" (context) | Wasewkit (son), Namowish (grandchild) |
| Grandchild name match | Nammeash (1672 — Anashuecot's eldest son) | **Namowish** (1659 — Tacommanan's grandchild) |

**Tag:** 🟡 PLAUSIBLE — HIGHLY PROBABLE

**Nammeash (1672) = Namowish (1659)** is the strongest link. Same phonetic root, same territory, same generation. If Namowish is Tacommanan's grandchild through a daughter, and that daughter is Anashuecot, then:

```
Tacommanan/Tocomminon
  └── Anashuecot (daughter, "Chiefe Sachem" 1672)
        ├── Nammeash/Namowish (eldest son)
        ├── Seccomp (son)
        └── Tyecuecsha (son)
  └── Wampkegge (sibling)
  └── Ompamiatt (sibling)
```

But also possible: Namowish is Tacommanan's grandchild through a SON (Wasewkit), and Anashuecot is a different child entirely. The deed doesn't say which child Namowish descends through.

**This is NOT resolved. Hold open.**

## PART 4: WHAT COULD ACTUALLY PRODUCE NEW DATA

These are the ONLY paths where new primary source evidence might exist and hasn't been searched:

### HIGH PRIORITY — Testable, accessible

1. **Anochetuckett 1687 Bartlett record** — Name phonetically closer to Anashuecot than any other variant. Full document NOT yet retrieved. Could show Anashuecot alive post-1676. [Tag: 🟡 PLAUSIBLE, source noted in memory]

2. **Tacommanan → Anashuecot genealogical link** — Is Namowish (1659) = Nammeash (1672)? If yes, this confirms the kin line. Search: any Narragansett genealogy that traces Tacommanan's children. Chapin may have it — need deeper read.

3. **Smith's household 1637–1650** — Who was at Cocumscussoc? Roger Williams's published letters. This maps John's arrival and status (settler vs. servant). Tests John Model IV.

4. **Freeman lists for John Greene** — When does he first appear independently? Arnold's Vital Records, Bartlett's Colony Records. If there's a gap between his arrival (~1637) and his first freeman appearance, that gap IS evidence of servitude.

5. **1661 Nisquitianxsett deed — full text** — Awashouse appears here with Nucom as interpreter. We have Potter's summary. Do we have the actual deed text? What does Awashouse's role/title look like in this earlier record?

### MEDIUM PRIORITY — Requires physical access or institutional response

6. **MA Archives Collection 30** — Physical manuscripts at Massachusetts State Archives. Kings Province jurisdiction files. If MA recorded dealings with Quidnessett sachems, it's here. PHYSICAL VISIT TARGET.

7. **Newport Court Martial records (1676)** — Awaswin appearance. RIHS or RI State Archives manuscripts. Your NYPL/JCB read is the current authority. PHYSICAL VISIT TARGET.

8. **North Kingstown Town Clerk — Land Evidence Book 1** — Ken Carlson confirmed custody. This is where the original 1682 deed may have been recorded separately from the Fones Record. PHYSICAL VISIT TARGET.

9. **Providence disposal committee records Vol. 15 pp. 151–154** — The actual captive distribution lists post-1676. If any Joan or any woman assigned to a Greene appears here, it's Model E evidence. Early Records of Providence, likely on Internet Archive.

### LOW PRIORITY — Possible but diminishing returns

10. **London Archives — St. George the Martyr burial register** — Mary Greene/Mary Jefferay. Splits Newport John from Quidnessett John. Email drafted but not sent.

11. **Hog Island deposition (Pilgrim Hall)** — Absalom independently called "Counselor unto the Narragansett Sachem." Two institutions, same title, decade apart. Quick web search.

12. **New Netherland records for Groen/Greene** — Tests John Model III (Dutch connection). Partially digitized. Low probability but untested.
