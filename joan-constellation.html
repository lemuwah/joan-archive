The Joan Archive · We Dwell Here

This is a map of relations — people, records, and open questions — centered on Joan Greene of Quidnessett and the possibility that she is Anashuecot, Narragansett sachem. The river below carries time forward; the land around it holds what the river alone cannot tell.

How to read this map

Every circle is a relation — a person whose story continues through descendants today. Squares are records — colonial documents that captured only what the writers chose to write. Diamonds are questions we still carry. Click anything to learn more. Drag to rearrange.

Tomaquag means beaver in Narragansett. Like the beaver, we build carefully, repair what is broken, and tend the water so the next seven generations can thrive.
