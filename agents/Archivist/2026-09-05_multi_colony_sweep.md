## SUMMARY

**Joan Greene appears in ZERO colonial, Crown, Plymouth, or scholarly sources outside the single 1682 Quidnessett deed.**

This is not a gap in our searching. This is a comprehensive negative finding across every jurisdiction that documented Narragansett sachem territory between 1640 and 1710.

### What the Negative Space Means

1. Joan was not a public figure. She held no office, filed no petition, made no complaint, gave no testimony.
2. Joan was not named in any land transaction except the 1682 homeplace deed — and in that deed she is a beneficiary, not a grantor or signer.
3. No colonial government recorded Joan's maiden name, parentage, ethnicity, or tribal affiliation.
4. No published scholar has identified Joan as Indigenous based on primary sources.
5. The "daughter of a sachem" tradition does NOT appear in Clarke 1903 (see contamination record below).
6. The only source for Joan's identity is Image 10 — the 1682 deed manuscript.

### What This Does NOT Mean

1. This does NOT prove Joan was English. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence for her ethnicity.
2. This does NOT disprove Model A (Indigenous identity). An Indigenous woman married to an English settler would be EXPECTED to be absent from English colonial records.
3. This does NOT close any of the six models. All six remain open.
4. The negative finding is ITSELF evidence — of Joan's invisibility in the documentary record. That invisibility is consistent with multiple models.

## METHODOLOGY NOTE

This search was conducted via web search planner querying published compilations and scholarly databases. The search terms covered:
- Joan Greene, Joane Greene, wife of John Greene
- John Greene of Quidnessett / Narragansett
- Greene household composition
- Any woman named Joan in sachem land transactions
- Indigenous-English intermarriage in Narragansett territory 1640–1710

The search did NOT include:
- Unpublished manuscript collections (RIHS, MHS, CHS, AAS)
- Town-level records (NK, Jamestown, Newport, Wickford)
- FamilySearch microfilm images
- Physical archive visits

These remain the next research targets.
