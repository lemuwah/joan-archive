## Proof Step 1: The Winthrop Papers — Joan's Only Named Appearance Before 1682

### What Winthrop Says (Primary Source)
**Source:** John Winthrop, *The Journal of John Winthrop, 1630–1649*, ed. Richard S. Dunn, James Savage, and Laetitia Yeandle (Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 1996).

**Entry dated December 14, 1638:**
> "one Greene (who hath married the wife of one Beggerly, whose husband is living, and no divorce, etc.)"

### What This Proves
- A man named "Greene" married a woman who was previously the wife of a man named "Beggerly"
- Beggerly (the first husband) was still alive at the time of the marriage
- No divorce had been obtained — this was a colonial scandal
- The woman is NOT named. Winthrop calls her "the wife of one Beggerly"
- The man "Greene" is NOT given a first name. No qualifier ("of Quidnessett," "surgeon") is provided

### What This Does NOT Prove
- ❌ That "Greene" = John Greene of Quidnessett (circumstantial identification only)
- ❌ That the woman's first name was "Joan" (Winthrop does not name her)
- ❌ That "Beggerly" was the woman's maiden name (it was her first husband's name)
- ❌ That this woman was ever called "Joan Beggarly" as a personal name

### Verification Status
- ✅ Winthrop's journal: PRIMARY SOURCE. The Harvard critical edition is authoritative.
- ⚠️ The identification "one Greene" = John Greene of Quidnessett: CIRCUMSTANTIAL. Based on: (a) timing — December 1638 fits the settlement period, (b) the 1636 court memorandum about a woman's divorce petition in the same community. No primary source explicitly links them.

## Proof Step 3: Alice Daniels "Beggerly" — The La Mance Conflation Target

### The La Mance Claim (CONTAMINATED)
La Mance (1904) and subsequent secondary sources claim: "Joan Beggarly" = "Alice Daniels" = wife of Surgeon John Greene of Warwick.

### Why This Fails — The Death Date
- **Alice Daniels Beggerly:** Secondary sources place her death at approximately **1643/44**.
- **Surgeon John Greene of Warwick:** Died by **7 January 1658/59** (will probated). His wife was Joanne TATTERSHALL (married 4 November 1619, St. Thomas Church, Salisbury, Wiltshire).
- **Joan (Unknown) of Quidnessett:** Alive in **March 1682** — receiving a life-estate annuity in the home-place deed.

### The Timeline Impossibility
If Alice Daniels Beggerly died c. 1643/44, she cannot be the Joan who appears in a 1682 deed. **A dead woman cannot receive a life-estate.** This is a hard chronological kill.

### What About "Beggarly" as the Surgeon's Wife's Name?
**CRITICAL NEW POLICY (2026-09-01):** We CANNOT assign "Beggarly" as the Surgeon's wife's maiden name without finding the paperwork.

- The Surgeon's documented wife is **Joanne Tattershall** (parish record: St. Thomas, Salisbury, 4 Nov 1619).
- The name "Beggerly" comes from Winthrop's journal, where the woman is called "the wife of one Beggerly" — meaning Beggerly was her FIRST HUSBAND's surname, not her maiden name.
- Whether the Surgeon married Joanne Tattershall AND ALSO married a woman previously married to a Beggerly is a SEPARATE question. No primary source places both marriages on the same man.
- La Mance's error was collapsing: Tattershall (Surgeon's parish-documented wife) + Beggerly (a first-husband surname from Winthrop) + Daniels (another surname from unknown provenance) into one figure and calling her "Joan Beggarly."

### Verification Status
- ✅ Tattershall marriage: PRIMARY SOURCE (St. Thomas parish register, Salisbury). Verified by Great Migration Study.
- ✅ Surgeon's death: PRIMARY SOURCE (will probated 7 Jan 1658/59, Warwick).
- ⚠️ Alice Daniels death date (~1643/44): SECONDARY SOURCE. Needs independent verification. **Until we see the paperwork, this date is ⚠️ UNVERIFIED.**
- ⚠️ "Alice Daniels" as any Greene wife: NO PRIMARY SOURCE located by this archive. The name "Alice Daniels" does not appear in any primary document the archive holds.
- ❌ "Joan Beggarly" as a combined identity: FABRICATED. La Mance construct with no single primary source behind it.

## Proof Step 4: Joan (Unknown) Greene of Quidnessett — What Actually Exists

### The Only Verified Appearance
**Source:** March 24, 1681/82 home-place deed. John Greene of Quidnessett divides land between sons Daniel and James.

- Joan is named as John's wife
- She receives 30 shillings/year after John's death, "dureing her naturall life"
- Whether she was a co-grantor (Bates says yes) or beneficiary only (Worth/NK MS say beneficiary) is CONTRADICTION C-1 — unresolved
- No maiden name given
- No family of origin named
- No prior document names her
- No subsequent document names her
- F.L. Greene (1894): "nothing further is known about her"

### What Separates Her from the Surgeon's Wife
1. **Different husband.** John Greene of Quidnessett ≠ Surgeon John Greene of Warwick (Two-John Firewall: chronological impossibility, geographic separation, Y-DNA divergence)
2. **Different timeline.** Joan of Quidnessett is alive in 1682. Surgeon's wife Joanne Tattershall married in 1619 and came to RI in 1635. If Tattershall was born c. 1600, she would be 82 in 1682 — possible but she is documented under the Surgeon's family line, not the Quidnessett line.
3. **No connecting document.** No primary source places Joan of Quidnessett in the Surgeon's family, in Warwick, or in the Tattershall lineage.

## Proof Step 5: The Verification Standard — Tightened 2026-09-01

### The New Rule
After the Pawtuxet citation kill and the mother-clause correction, the archive adopts a stricter verification standard:

> **Nothing is VERIFIED (🟢) until the archive sees the primary source for itself.** Secondary sources — including Bates (1918), F.L. Greene (1894), Austin (1887), Gibbs, and all algorithmic trees — are treated as LEADS, not evidence. A secondary source citation moves a claim to ⚠️ UNVERIFIED, not 🟢 VERIFIED.

> **🟢 VERIFIED** = the archive holds or has directly read the primary source document (original manuscript, certified transcription, or critical edition).
> **⚠️ UNVERIFIED** = a secondary source cites or paraphrases the claim, but the archive has not independently confirmed it.
> **⚠️ SUSPENDED** = a citation existed but has been invalidated or cannot be located.
> **❌ KILLED** = the claim has been definitively disproven.

### What This Means for "Beggarly"
- The surname "Beggarly" appears in Winthrop (primary source) as a FIRST HUSBAND's surname: "the wife of one Beggerly."
- Whether this woman later became the Surgeon's wife, or Quidnessett John's wife, or someone else entirely, is NOT established by Winthrop.
- Assigning "Beggarly" to any specific woman as a maiden name, or to the Surgeon's marriage, requires PRIMARY SOURCE DOCUMENTATION that the archive does not hold.
- Until that paperwork is found: **Joan (Unknown) Greene of Quidnessett. Maiden name: Unknown. Previous marriage: Unknown. "Nothing further is known about her."**
