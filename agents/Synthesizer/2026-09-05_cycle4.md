## What Changed Today

### 1. Joan's Documentary Footprint — Reduced to One

Joan appears in ONE document across all of Worthington's Rhode Island Land Evidences Vol. I (1648–1696). Page 173. March 1682. That's the complete colony-level record.

Every other deed we've been citing (Clark-to-Brinley 1685, James-to-Wightman 1695/96, Caleb Carr 1651) is NOT in Worthington. Those are Bates citations to local town records we haven't read.

### 2. John Has Brothers — New Family Structure

The 1675 deed (Worthington p. 102) reveals John gave land to "my two Brothers Mr James Greene and Mr Thomas Greene." This means:
- There are TWO James Greenes: uncle (brother) and nephew (son)
- The compiled genealogies that list "James" without specifying which one are contaminated
- Every "James Greene" reference in the archive needs an audit

### 3. No Birth Records Exist — For Any Quidnessett Child

Every child is reconstructed from deeds, wills, and marriages. Zero baptisms. Zero birth entries. The compilations (Austin, Arnold, FLG) present estimated dates as if they're facts. They're inferences.

### 4. Smith's Household — A Ghost

John "lived with" Smith at Cocumscussoc. No other person at the trading post is named in primary sources except Smith himself. No women documented. The household is invisible.

### 5. Anochetuckett = Place, Not Person

The 1687 Bartlett reference is a river name, not a living sachem. Confirms territorial association but not personal survival post-1676.
