## The Honest State of the Archive

### What We Know (Documented Facts)
- Joan Greene exists in exactly 2 documents (March 1682, May 1682)
- She has zero pre-1682 documentation of any kind
- No alternative English Joan has been found in 4 colonies
- The only proposed alternative (Beggarly) is a different woman married to a different man, dead 38 years too early
- The "or to her mother" clause has no English legal parallel
- The Quidnessett sachem succession gap opened in 1668 (Scuttup's death), 4 years before Anashuecot appears
- Weunquesh exercised female land authority in RI courts in 1679
- John Greene actively engaged with the Narragansett community (1676 sloop, 1679 Conservator)
- A witness named Awassuocitt (phonetically similar to Anashuecot) appears in a 1654 deed
- Narragansett culture is matrilineal (Tomaquag Museum)
- Colonial records systematically erased Indigenous women's names (Tomaquag Museum)

### What We Don't Know (Gaps)
- Joan's identity — no document says Joan = Anashuecot
- Joan's maiden name, origin, family
- Who "her mother" was
- The genealogical link between Scuttup/Quequaquenuit and Anashuecot
- Whether Awassuocitt = Anashuecot
- Joan's mark — no visual found despite search

### The Honest Model Comparison

**Model A (Joan was Narragansett)** explains Joan's pattern with one premise: she was an Indigenous woman whose name was encoded in English legal forms. This is consistent with:
- Her total absence from English records
- The matrilineal inheritance clause
- The survival anomaly
- The 1682 timing
- Documented Indigenous erasure patterns

**Model B (Joan was an unknown English woman)** requires 3–4 independent anomalies to all coincidentally apply to one woman:
1. Total absence from ALL English record systems (unique among Quidnessett wives)
2. A matrilineal inheritance clause that has no English legal basis
3. The survival anomaly (possibly survivorship bias)
4. The pre-war succession coincidence

Model A is more parsimonious. Model B is not impossible but requires more special pleading.

**Neither model is proven.** The archive documents what the colonial record says and doesn't say.
