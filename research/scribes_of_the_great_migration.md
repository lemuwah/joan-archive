# Scribes of the Great Migration — Whose Handwriting Holds Joan's World

**Status:** ACTIVE reference — feeds the Greene Net and Task 6.75 search queue
**Date:** 2026-09-09
**Purpose:** The people who *wrote down* New England, 1630–1700, often kept personal notebooks, diaries, letterbooks, and casebooks beyond the official record. Those artifacts are digitized today. A wife invisible in deeds may appear in a physician's casebook, a trader's letter, or a minister's diary as "Goodwife Greene," "John Greene's wife," or nothing but a place and a date — which is why every entry below lists *what to search*, not just who wrote.

**Honesty rule:** None of these entries is evidence about Joan. Each is a **search lead** tagged like any other claim. Repository pointers name the holding institution; verify the exact volume/URL before citing (Law 7). Entries marked ⚠️ need verification of the digitization claim itself.
