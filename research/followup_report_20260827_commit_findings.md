## Executive Summary

- **Anochetuckett is a river, not a person** — distinguished from Anashuecot (1672 sachem) through Algonquian morphology. -tucket = river; -cot = area/person. The river runs through Joan's home territory, ~1 mile from Wickford/Cocumscussoc. [1][2][3]
- **Nath. Greene remains unidentified** — not in Bates's children list. Known Warwick Nathaniel (b. 1679) too young at age 8 to witness 1687 deed. Three candidates, none confirmed. [4][5]
- **Coginaquand deed date discrepancy** — 1687 Bartlett recording likely re-recording of 1659 Atherton deed, not new transaction. Andros administration required re-registration. [6][7][8]
- **Coginaquand's relationship to Canonicus more complex than "son"** — Our Beloved Kin identifies him as "cousin to Quaiapin's late husband Mixanno" (Mixanno was Canonicus's son); other sources call him "brother of Miantonomo" (Canonicus's nephew). [9][10]

## Finding 1: Anochetuckett — A River, Not a Person

| Name | Category | Meaning | Source |
|------|----------|---------|--------|
| Anashuecot | Person | Sachem title | 1672 Fones Purchase |
| Anochetuckett | Place | River; "at the end of the tidal stream" | 1687 Bartlett Vol. 3 |

Morpheme -tucket/-tukett = tidal river/stream (cf. Pawtucket, Nantucket). Morpheme -cot = continuous area. Confirmed by Cape Ann History linguistic analysis.

Modern Annaquatucket River: North Kingstown, west-southwest of Wickford, toward Bissel Cove. Southern boundary of Cocumscussoc. Eight historical spelling variants documented.

## Finding 2: Nath. Greene — Unidentified

Witnesses 1687 Coginaquand/Namcock deed alongside John Vial, Thomas Stanton, John Stanton, John Farniside.

**Three candidates:**
1. Warwick Greene line ("More Plausible") — but known Nathaniel b. 1679 = age 8 in 1687
2. Unknown Quidnessett Greene ("Less Plausible") — requires undocumented child
3. Unrelated Greene ("Possible") — common surname

Five-John Firewall intact. Identity remains open.

## Finding 3: Coginaquand Date Discrepancy

**1687 Bartlett entry vs. established 1659 Atherton deed:**

**(A) Re-recording (More Likely):** Fones Record = 1703 compilation. Andros administration (1686-89) required re-registration. Language mirrors 1659 original. Witnesses = recording-witnesses, not original.

**(B) New transaction (Less Likely):** Requires Coginaquand alive at age 80+, still exercising authority 11 years post-war.

**Coginaquand identity disputed:**
- Archive: "son of Canonicus"
- Our Beloved Kin: "cousin to Quaiapin's late husband Mixanno"
- Other sources: "brother of Miantonomo"

9 variant spellings documented. Atherton Company context: Roger Williams refused to translate, condemned as "unneighborly and unchristian Intrusion."

## Synthesis

All three notes map the same Cocumscussoc-Quidnessett-Namcock Neck land corridor (~9 miles). Andros-era re-recording raises question: **Were Joan's 1682 deeds part of broader administrative reorganization?**
