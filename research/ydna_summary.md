## Clade Context: R-Z255

- R-Z255 is a Y-DNA subclade of R1b, branching from R-Z16423 approximately **2250 BCE**
- Most recent common ancestor of present-day R-Z255 men estimated around **100 BCE**
- Known descendant lineages include R-Z16437, R-L159 (L159.2/S169.2), and R-BY17794
- R-Z255 is most commonly found in the **United Kingdom and Ireland** — consistent with an Insular Celtic / Irish-British Isles association
- Source: FTDNA Discover (https://discover.familytreedna.com/y-dna/R-Z255/)

## Modal Match

The STR profile matches the **Uí Dúnlainge / O'Byrne / O'Toole** modal — an Irish Sea cluster associated with medieval Irish dynasties.

**This does NOT match English Greene families.**

The R-Z255 cluster is concentrated in Ireland, western Britain, and the Irish Sea corridor. The English Greene families of record (including the Surgeon John Greene line from Salisbury, Wiltshire) would be expected to carry haplogroups more common in southern England.
