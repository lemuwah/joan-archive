## 1. Rhode Island State Archives

**Inquiry dates:** February–March 2026
**Subject:** Location of earliest land evidence volumes for Narragansett Country (Wickford / Quidnessett)

### Confirmed facts:
- **Land Evidence Book 1 is held by the North Kingstown town clerk** — not the State Archives. This is the volume containing the earliest deeds for the Quidnessett region.
- **The Fones Record has been digitized** (jpeg & pdf formats exist).
- **The 1672 Anashuecot deed is on page 90** of the Fones Record.
- **The three-John structure is evident from the records.** The Archives confirmed that three individuals named John Green/e existed as contemporaries during the period — John Greene of Warwick (the Surgeon's son), John Greene of Newport, and John Greene of Quidnessett.

### Open items:
- Request for high-resolution scans of the 1672 Anashuecot deed signatures and the 1682 Joan Greene deed marks for visual comparison — **no response to this specific question.**
- The researcher's email was forwarded to a third party without consent after the researcher explicitly requested it not be forwarded. This is documented as a **methodology note on researcher ethics and institutional trust**, not as a complaint. It illustrates a real hazard for independent researchers: sharing work-in-progress with institutions carries risks that affiliated researchers are insulated from.

### Status: PARTIALLY CLOSED — custodial questions answered, scan request still open.
