## The Claim

A deed dated **May 19, 1682** in which John Greene and his wife Joan sell Pawtuxet land-rights to William Carpenter for £10. Joan gives her "free & voluntary consent" and signs by "her marke." Witnesses: John Wickes and Samuel Gorton.

Cited as: **"RI Land Evidence, vol. VII, pp. 177–178"**

If real, this is the ONLY record where:
- Joan's first name appears explicitly
- Joan signs with "her marke"
- Joan gives "free & voluntary consent"
- Joan is an active legal agent (not just named by role)

### Phase 2: Citation Identification (2026-08-31)

**Bartlett Vol. VII = WRONG CENTURY.** Bartlett's printed *Records of the Colony of Rhode Island and Providence Plantations* Volume VII covers **1770–1776** (Revolutionary era). Available on Internet Archive and HathiTrust. A 1682 deed cannot be on pp. 177–178 of this volume.

**RI State Archives "Land & Public Notary Records" Vol. VII = WRONG SERIES.** Their Vol. 7 covers Public Notary records, 1758–1790. Not land deeds.

**Worthington's RI Land Evidences Vol. I (1648–1696) = deed NOT INCLUDED.** The printed abstracts cover the right time period but do not contain this deed. Either Worthington skipped it, or it's in a different manuscript volume.

### Phase 3: Bates Citation Chain (2026-08-31) — 🚨 CRITICAL NEGATIVE FINDING

**Source:** Louise Prosser Bates, "John Greene of Newport and Narragansett," RIHS *Collections*, Vol. XI (1918), pp. 69–78 & 117–121; Vol. XII (1919), pp. 15–27. Read from Internet Archive scan of RIHS Collections Vols. 11–14.

Bates is the most authoritative secondary source on John Greene of Quidnessett. Her article covers his full life, land transactions, and family.

**What Bates says about 1682 (verbatim from p. 169 of the scan):**

> *"In 1682 he seems to have begun to settle up his affairs. On February 4, 1682, Edward Greenman acknowledged, for himself and brother David, the deed he had given Greene to land in Newport in 1647. Greene and his wife Joan deeded to their sons, Daniel² and James,² land in Quidnisset in return for thirty shillings a year paid by each of them so long as either parent lived. This was March 24, 1681/2. He left a will, as is shown by the deeds of his son Edward, but it was doubtless destroyed in the North Kingstown fire. He died before September 25, 1685."*

**BATES DOES NOT MENTION:**
- The May 19, 1682 Pawtuxet deed
- William Carpenter as a grantee
- Joan giving "free & voluntary consent"
- Joan signing by "her marke"
- "RI Land Evidence vol. VII pp. 177–178"
- Any Pawtuxet land transaction at all

**Conclusion:** The Pawtuxet deed citation does NOT originate from Bates. The citation chain is broken at the most authoritative link.

## What We Know About the Deed Details

Multiple secondary sources (genealogical compilations, online databases) cite the same details:
- John Greene and Joan Greene → William Carpenter
- £10 consideration
- Witnesses: John Wickes and Samuel Gorton
- Joan's "free & voluntary consent" and "her marke"
- Dated May 19, 1682
- Cited as "RI Land Evidence vol. VII pp. 177–178"

These details are internally consistent and specific. But ALL may derive from a single unverified secondary source that has been copied without independent verification.

## Remaining Leads

### Where "Vol. VII pp. 177–178" might actually be:
1. **A Providence town-level deed book** — Pawtuxet was under Providence jurisdiction. Providence colonial deed books use their own volume numbering. "Vol. VII" could be a Providence deed book.
2. **An unpublished RI State Archives manuscript** not in their online catalog. The original manuscripts are described as "four ancient volumes" of deeds — but additional volumes of recorded deeds may exist beyond these four.
3. **FamilySearch Film 564389** — RI Land Evidences Vol. I original manuscript. Worthington's abstracts may have skipped this deed even though the manuscript contains it.
4. **Peirce Manuscripts** (FamilySearch films 22291–22292) — handwritten deed abstracts.
5. **RIHS MSS 1210, Box 2, Folder 13** (Kettelle Papers — "Greenes of Quidnessett")

### Where the CITATION might originate:
1. **F.L. Greene, *The Greene Family and Its Branches* (1894)** — Did this earlier compilation cite the Pawtuxet deed? If so, what source did IT use?
2. **Austin, *Genealogical Dictionary of Rhode Island* (1887)** — Referenced by Bates. May contain the deed.
3. **Updike, *A History of the Episcopal Church in Narragansett* (1907/1937)** — Covers Narragansett land transactions extensively.
4. **Post-Bates genealogical compilations** — The citation may originate from a mid-20th-century source copying from an unknown manuscript.

## The 1658 Connection

The verified **1658 Pawtuxet deed** ("John Greene Junr Warwick" → William Carpenter) is a SEPARATE transaction. Different grantors, different witnesses (John Greene, Ezekiel Holliman, Christopher Unthank), different terms (no Joan, no £10, no consent clause).

BUT: If the 1682 Pawtuxet deed is real and says John Greene sold "inherited Pawtuxet land," the question becomes: inherited from whom? Through Joan? Through the Surgeon's line? Through a separate acquisition? The 1658 deed shows the Surgeon's son selling Pawtuxet land to the SAME William Carpenter 24 years earlier. Coincidence requires investigation.
