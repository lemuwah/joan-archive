- **Problem:** Citation "RI Land Evidence vol. VII pp. 177–178" is **DEFINITIVELY DEAD** — those pages contain 1773 Gaspee commission records (Chief Justice Smythe's letter + commissioners' cover to Lord Dartmouth). Bates (most authoritative source) does not mention this deed.

### 19. BATES CITATION CHAIN
- **Status:** ✅ RESOLVED (2026-08-31) — **NEGATIVE**
- **Result:** Bates 1918–19 (RIHS Collections XI–XII) does NOT mention the Pawtuxet deed. She goes directly from March 1682 home-place deed → will → death. No William Carpenter. No "her marke." No Pawtuxet.
- **Bates's 1682 text (verbatim):** "Greene and his wife Joan deeded to their sons, Daniel² and James,² land in Quidnisset in return for thirty shillings a year paid by each of them so long as either parent lived. This was March 24, 1681/2."
- **Bates's children list:** Lt. John², Henry², Daniel², James², Benjamin², Sarah² (married Thomas Flounders).
- **Next targets for citation chain:** See #21.

### 19c. JOHN SR.'S DEATH DATE — F.L. GREENE vs BATES (NEW 2026-09-01)
- **What:** F.L. Greene 1894 p. 10 has John Sr. signing an address to the King 16 July 1686 and witnessing a deed 13 May 1692, "probably" dying before the 1696 freemen list. Bates 1918 p. 74 has him dead before 25 Sept 1685 on the strength of Joseph Clark's Conanicut deed to Francis Brinley ("John Greene, sr., of Narragansett, now deceased" — Jamestown Records). Both cannot be true of the same man.
- **Pulled 2026-09-01:** Bates pp. 74 and 77 and F.L. Greene p. 10 page images are now in `images/` and written up at `primary_sources/bates-1918.md`. Bates explicitly un-merges the later John Greenes: Lieut. John² of Newport (alive 1690) and a John Greene *alias* Clarke of East Greenwich (b. 6 June 1651, m. Abigail Wardwell) — whom F.L. Greene lists as John Sr.'s son "John, Jr." So the 1686/1692 acts are probably one of those men, and the "1695" death is probably a son's record. **Still unseen:** the Clark→Brinley deed itself, the 1686 address, the 1692 deed, and Edward Greene's deeds reciting his father's will. **Held open under Law 1** — Bates is the better-argued source, not a verified one.

### 21. TRACE THE ORPHAN CITATION — NEW
- **What:** The "vol. VII pp. 177–178" citation does NOT come from Bates. Where DOES it come from? It may originate from a post-Bates genealogical compilation.
- **Targets (in order):**
  1. **F.L. Greene, *The Greene Family and Its Branches* (1894)** — pre-dates Bates. If this book cites the deed, it predates and is independent of Bates.
  2. **Austin, *Genealogical Dictionary of Rhode Island* (1887)** — Referenced by Bates on p. 351. Widely used by later compilers.
  3. **Updike, *A History of the Episcopal Church in Narragansett* (1907/1937)** — Covers Narragansett land transactions.
  4. **Post-Bates compilations** — *Genealogies of Rhode Island Families* (1983), Ancestry/FamilySearch source citations, other genealogical databases.
- **Goal:** Find the EARLIEST source that cites "vol. VII pp. 177–178" and determine whether it references an actual manuscript or is circular.
- **Status:** PENDING — start with F.L. Greene 1894 on Internet Archive.
