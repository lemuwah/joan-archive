### 17. PAWTUXET DEED PRIMARY SOURCE HUNT
- **What:** May 19, 1682 deed, John & Joan Greene → William Carpenter, £10, witnesses John Wickes & Samuel Gorton. "Free & voluntary consent." "Her marke."
- **Problem:** Cited as "RI Land Evidence vol. VII pp. 177–178" — but this citation doesn't resolve to any known digitized volume. Bartlett Vol. VII = 1770s. RI State Archives Vol. VII = maritime/notary. Worthington abstracts = deed NOT FOUND.
- **What's been searched:** See `theory/proof_pieces/09_pawtuxet_problem.md` for exhaustive digital search results.
- **Best lead:** Bates 1918–19 article (RIHS Collections XI–XII). Read her exact citation for this deed and trace it backward.
- **Second lead:** "Vol. VII" likely refers to a TOWN-LEVEL deed book. Check Providence City Hall colonial deed books.
- **Status:** 🔴 PRIORITY 1. The archive cannot claim 2 Joan appearances until this deed is found or killed.

### 18. 1658 CO-SIGNING CLUSTER — QUIDNESSETT JOHN + SURGEON'S SON
- **What:** The verified 1658 Pawtuxet deed has "John Greene Junr Warwick" (Surgeon's son) selling to William Carpenter. Witnesses include "John Greene" — which one?
- **Significance:** If the witnessing "John Greene" is Quidnessett John, this is the EARLIEST primary source linking the two lines directly. They co-sign the same deed in 1658.
- **Implications:**
  - If they're on the same deed, they knew each other. Were they related (different branches of the same family)? Or unrelated men in the same colonial network?
  - The Greene DNA Project shows genetic separation. If they co-sign, they're socially connected but genetically distinct.
  - Complicates the Five-John Firewall — can't cleanly separate Quidnessett from Surgeon's line if they share legal transactions.
  - 24 years later (1682), a "John Greene" sells "inherited Pawtuxet land" to the SAME William Carpenter. If Quidnessett John witnessed the 1658 sale, why does he later sell Pawtuxet land? Did he inherit it? Did he acquire it separately? Was it through Joan?
- **Where:** Get the full 1658 deed text — witness line, any identifying descriptors.
- **Status:** 🔴 HIGH PRIORITY. Blocks clean separation of John lines.

### 19. BATES CITATION CHAIN
- **What:** Louise Prosser Bates, "John Greene of Newport and Narragansett," RIHS *Collections* XI (1918) pp. 69–78 & 117–121, XII (1919) pp. 15–27.
- **Where:** Internet Archive (RIHS Collections Vols. 11–14 scan). Also reprinted in *Genealogies of Rhode Island Families* (1983), Vol. I, pp. 432–456.
- **Goal:** Find EXACTLY what she says about the May 1682 Pawtuxet deed and trace her source. Does the citation chain go back to a specific manuscript, or is it circular?

### 20. "RI LAND EVIDENCE VOL. VII" — WHICH PHYSICAL VOLUME?
- **What:** The citation "RI Land Evidence vol. VII pp. 177–178" appears in multiple secondary sources. But what IS Vol. VII?
- **Possibilities:**
  - Providence town-level deed book (colonial numbering)
  - Warwick town-level deed book
  - An unpublished manuscript volume at RI State Archives distinct from Worthington's printed series
  - A numbering convention used by 19th-century genealogists that doesn't map to any modern finding aid
- **Where:** Call Providence City Clerk, RI State Archives, or RIHS. Ask: "Do you have a colonial-era 'Land Evidence Volume VII' containing 1680s deeds?"
- **Status:** PENDING — may require phone call or in-person visit.
