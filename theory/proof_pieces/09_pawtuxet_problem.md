A systematic search of digitized sources on Internet Archive — including *Rhode Island Land Evidences Vol. I* (Worth), *Early Records of the Town of Providence*, and *History and Genealogy of the Carpenter Family* — **did not locate a verbatim transcription of a deed dated 19 May 1682 from John Greene to William Carpenter for £10 with Joan's consent.**

### What Was NOT Found:

- ❌ No deed dated **19 May 1682** in any searched volume
- ❌ No £10 consideration in any Greene-Carpenter transaction
- ❌ The word **"inherited"** does NOT appear in any located deed text
- ❌ The phrase **"free & voluntary consent"** from Joan does NOT appear in any located Pawtuxet deed
- ❌ No grantor clause reading "John Greene of Quidnessett" or "John Greene of Warwick" in a Pawtuxet context

### The Conflation Hypothesis

The search results suggest the "19 May 1682 Pawtuxet deed" as described in secondary sources **may be a conflation** of:
1. The **1658 John Greene Junr Warwick → William Carpenter** deed (Pawtuxet land)
2. The **March 1681/82 John and Joan Greene → James Greene** deed (Quidnessett land, Joan named)

If someone reading abstracts saw "John Greene" + "William Carpenter" + "Pawtuxet" in one entry (1658) and "John Greene" + "Joan" + "consent" in another (1682), they could have merged them into one "1682 Pawtuxet deed with Joan's consent."

⚠️ **Per Law 1:** This is a HYPOTHESIS, not a proven conflation. The deed may exist in undigitized sources.

## What Our Own Source Spine Says

The Joan Archive source spine (line 43) cites the Pawtuxet deed at:
> *"Records of RI & Providence Plantations, Vol. VII, pp. 177–178"*
> Status: 🟢 PROVEN

The 1682 deed lifecycle document (Node 3) also cites this source.

**BUT:** Vol. VII of the Colony Records is NOT the same as RI Land Evidences Vol. I. Worth's *Land Evidences* is an abstract compilation of land records from 1648–1696. The Colony Records (Bartlett edition) are the legislative/administrative records. These are different document sets.

### Possible Resolutions:

1. **The deed IS in Colony Records Vol. VII pp. 177–178** — a volume we haven't accessed. This would be definitive.
2. **The citation is wrong** — "Vol. VII" may be a mislabeled reference to a different volume.
3. **The deed IS in the Land Evidences but under a different abstract number or page** — Worth's OCR text may have garbled the entry.
4. **The deed was never in a printed source** — it may exist only in manuscript (NK Land Evidence, Peirce Manuscripts).

## The 1658 Deed — Verbatim Text (VERIFIED)

This IS located and verified:

> *"John Greene Junr Warwick Have sold unto William Carpenter of Pawtuxet in Providence all my right in the little neck of Land layinge below Pawtuxet Falls, all my right in that place comonly called the Vinyard, alsoe my right of Land as yett undevided from Packassett river Eastward downe to the Sea together with all the previleges of water 20th of November 1658"*

> *Witnesses: John Greene, Ezekel Holyman his X marke, Christopher Unthanke*

**This is definitively Surgeon John's son** (future Deputy Governor), selling to Carpenter. "Junr Warwick" = junior to his father in Warwick. No Joan. No £10. No "inherited."
