## CRITICAL FINDING: Occupasnetuxet ≠ Pawtuxet

These are **different, neighboring localities:**

| Property | Owner | How Acquired | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Occupasnetuxet / Greene's Hold** | Surgeon John Greene | Purchased from Miantonomi, Oct 1, 1642 | ~700-acre neck SOUTH of Pawtuxet |
| **Pawtuxet proprietary share** | "John Green junr." | Listed as purchaser, Nov 1648 | Pawtuxet Falls area — Warwick/Providence boundary |

Surgeon John's will bequeathed **Occupasnetuxet** to his son John. This is NOT the same land sold by our John and Joan in 1682. The 1682 deed sells **Pawtuxet** land.

**This distinction is vital.** The Pawtuxet land did NOT come from Surgeon John's will.

## The Pawtuxet Chain of Title — Reconstructed

### Transaction 1: The Original Pawtuxet Purchase (November 1648)

The "purchassers of Pawtuxett" are recorded as:
- Richard Waterman
- Ezekiel Hollyman
- William Harris
- **John Green junr.**
- William Field (for Richard Parker)
- Richard Harker

Source: *Proceedings of the First General Assembly, and also of the Court of Commissioners for the Province of Providence Plantations* (Gutenberg 63358)

### Who Is the 1648 "John Green junr."?

| Candidate | Age in 1648 | "Junior" to whom? | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Surgeon John's son (future Deputy Gov.)** | 28 | Junior to Surgeon John (alive in Warwick) | ✅ PROBABLE — standard "Jr" usage, Warwick social network matches Hollyman, Harris, Waterman |
| **Our John of Quidnessett** | ~42 | Junior to... whom? | ⚠️ POSSIBLE but problematic — he's OLDER than the Deputy Gov. candidate; no known "Senior" to be junior to unless there's a THIRD John |

**The standard identification:** "John Green junr." in a 1648 Pawtuxet/Warwick context is Surgeon John's eldest surviving son. Surgeon John was alive and active in Warwick — the "junr." distinguishes father from son.

**But here's the problem for the standard identification:** Surgeon John's son sold his Pawtuxet rights to Carpenter in **1658** (the near-verbatim deed we have). If Surgeon John's son already sold those rights in 1658, **what was left to sell in 1682?**

### Transaction 2: The 1658 Sale (John Greene Junr Warwick → William Carpenter)

Near-verbatim text recovered:

> *"John Greene Junr Warwick Have sold unto William Carpenter of Pawtuxet in Providence all my right in the little neck of Land layinge below Pawtuxet Falls, all my right in that place comonly called the Vinyard, alsoe my right of Land as yett undevided from Packassett river Eastward downe to the Sea together with all the previleges of water 20th of November 1658"*

> *Witnesses: John Greene, Ezekel Holyman his X marke, Christopher Unthanke*

This conveyed:
- The little neck below Pawtuxet Falls
- "The Vineyard"
- Undivided land from Packassett River eastward to the sea
- Water privileges

**"All my right"** — this is a total alienation. If this is the SAME Pawtuxet share from 1648, it was fully conveyed to Carpenter in 1658.

### 🚨 THE KEY QUESTION: What Did Our John Sell in 1682?

If Surgeon John's son sold "all my right" in Pawtuxet to Carpenter in 1658, what "inherited Pawtuxet land" does our John sell to the SAME Carpenter 24 years later?

**Three possibilities:**

**A. A DIFFERENT Pawtuxet parcel** — our John had a separate Pawtuxet holding acquired independently. The 1682 deed may describe a different piece of land entirely. CANNOT BE CONFIRMED without the verbatim 1682 bounds.

**B. A residual interest** — the 1658 sale conveyed personal rights but not an inherited reversionary interest. Our John inherited a claim that survived the 1658 sale. Legally unusual but not impossible in colonial RI.

**C. "John Green junr." in 1648 was NOT Surgeon John's son — it was our John all along.**

## Part 2: Testing Possibility C — Was "John Green junr." (1648) Our John?

### Evidence FOR:

1. **The 1682 deed calls the land "inherited."** If our John inherited Pawtuxet land, the simplest explanation is that he was the ORIGINAL proprietor (1648) or inherited from one.

2. **The 1658 sale is explicitly by "John Greene Junr WARWICK."** If our John was at Richard Smith's trading post (Narragansett Country), he was NOT "of Warwick." The 1658 seller is localized to Warwick — that's the Deputy Governor candidate.

3. **Our John's timeline doesn't conflict.** In 1648 he was ~42 years old, living in the Newport/Narragansett area. Pawtuxet is accessible. He could have been a Pawtuxet proprietor while living further south.

4. **"Junior" without a Senior.** If our John is unrelated to Surgeon John, what makes him "junr"? Possibilities:
   - "Junior" in age or seniority among the group (not necessarily to a father)
   - "Junior" to Surgeon John by social convention (both named John Greene, same colony, so the older/more prominent one gets "Senior")
   - There's a THIRD "Senior" John Greene we haven't found

5. **DNA.** The Y-DNA divergence (R-Z255 Irish Sea vs. English Greene modal) is consistent with our John being an unrelated man named "John Green" who was called "junior" relative to the more established Surgeon John — not because they were father/son.

### Evidence AGAINST:

1. **The 1648 Pawtuxet proprietor list is a WARWICK document.** The other proprietors (Waterman, Hollyman, Harris, Harker) are all Warwick/Providence men. Our John was associated with Smith's trading post in Narragansett Country. Why is he in a Warwick land group?

2. **Surgeon John's son is the simpler "Junior" explanation.** Father alive in Warwick + son named John = "John Green junr."

3. **If our John was a 1648 Pawtuxet proprietor, why did Surgeon John's son sell Pawtuxet rights in 1658?** Either (a) there were TWO Greene shares at Pawtuxet (possible but needs evidence), or (b) they're the same share being sold at different times by different people (messy).

## Part 4: Hostile Review

### The hostile reviewer says:

**1.** The "inherited" language comes from an **abstract**, not the verbatim deed. Until pp. 177–178 are read, we don't know if "inherited" is the original word or a paraphrase. **This is the #1 priority.**

**2.** Possibility C (our John = 1648 proprietor) is **attractive but circular.** We're identifying the 1648 "John Green junr." as our John BECAUSE we need to explain the 1682 inheritance. That's backwards. An independent identification is needed.

**3.** The 1658 witness list includes **"John Greene"** (no qualifier) alongside "Ezekel Holyman his X marke" and "Christopher Unthanke." Is this witnessing "John Greene" Surgeon John himself, or a third party? **We can't tell.**

**4.** The locational expansion is REAL but cuts both ways. If our John moved between Newport, Pawtuxet, and Quidnessett, he was deeply embedded in ENGLISH property networks. This makes it HARDER to argue he was marginal or on the frontier edge. Counter-argument: these are exactly the networks where a man with cross-cultural connections would thrive.

**5.** Two men named "John Greene" owning Pawtuxet land at different times, selling to the SAME buyer (Carpenter), could simply mean Carpenter was assembling a large holding from multiple sources over decades. No mystery required.
