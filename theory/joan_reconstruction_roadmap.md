## Purpose

This roadmap tracks every proof piece relevant to the identity of Joan, wife of John Greene of Quidnessett (1682 deed, Fones Record p.90). Each item is evaluated against three models:

- **Model A:** Joan = Anashuecot (Narragansett woman from the 1672 Fones Purchase deed)
- **Model B:** Joan = unknown English woman (null hypothesis — no documented origin)
- **Model C:** Joan = Alice Daniels "Beggerly" (La Mance 1904 — ELIMINATED)

Model C is retained for documentary completeness. It has been eliminated by primary source evidence: Benedict Arnold's records and Winthrop Papers establish that Alice Daniels married John Greene of Warwick (a different John), not the Quidnessett John. Timeline impossible for the 1682 deed.

Per Law 3 (No Early Exclusion), Model B remains active until positive evidence identifies Joan or until every plausible English origin pathway has been searched and returned empty.

Per Law 4 (Transparent AI Caution), all AI-generated analysis is flagged. No AI inference is presented as evidence.

## Laws Governing This Roadmap

1. **Law 1 — Indigenous Record Ethics:** Treat every Indigenous name, mark, and deed reference with the understanding that these are sovereign acts, not quaint footnotes.
2. **Law 2 — No Narrative Smoothing:** Do not fill gaps with plausible stories. If we don't know, we say we don't know.
3. **Law 3 — No Early Exclusion:** The null hypothesis (Joan = unknown English woman) stays active until eliminated by evidence, not assumption.
4. **Law 4 — Transparent AI Caution:** All AI-generated analysis is flagged. AI can suggest directions; AI cannot provide evidence.
5. **Law 5 — Respect Living Relationships:** No names of living researchers or contacts without explicit permission. Institutional confirmations only.
