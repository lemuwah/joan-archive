### CRITICAL CORRECTION: The 1695/96 Deed

Earlier today I stated this was **Benjamin to James**. That was wrong. Bates says it's **James Greene to George Wightman** — James is SELLING, not receiving. 18¼ acres in the Quidnessett Purchase, bounded by brothers Benjamin and Edward.

**Why this matters for Joan:** If James is selling land that carried the annuity obligation, either:
- Joan is dead by 1695/96 and the obligation expired (the annuity was for her natural life)
- Joan is alive and the annuity transfers with the land to Wightman
- The annuity was already satisfied or discharged

**The deed text is the answer.** If Joan is mentioned as still living = alive 1695/96. If not mentioned = probably dead. This deed is in Worthington's abstracts but we haven't read the abstract entry or the manuscript.
