### Processing Note — CRITICAL
The finding aid says:
> "Manuscript Greene genealogy, began by George S. Greene in 1835, with notes through 1915. Later published as *Greenes of Rhode Island...* See Reference Collection."

This manuscript is at RIHS but in the **Reference Collection**, not in MSS 460. It was published as:

**Clarke, Louise Brownell. *The Greenes of Rhode Island, with Historical Records of English Ancestry, 1534–1902.* New York, 1903.**

This published book is almost certainly the authoritative Greene genealogy that Bates used. George S. Greene began it in 1835, 66 years before publication. Louise Brownell Clarke completed and published it after his death in 1899.

**If any family tradition about Joan exists, it's in this book.**
