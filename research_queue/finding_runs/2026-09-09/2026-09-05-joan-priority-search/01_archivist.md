## THE NEW JAMES PROBLEM

The 1675 deed names James as John's BROTHER.
The 1682 deed names James as John and Joan's SON.

These are two different people. But every compiled genealogy treats "James Greene" as one person. This is a contamination trap:

- If James the brother's deeds get mixed with James the son's deeds, every claim downstream is corrupted
- The "Mr" title for the brother suggests higher status than the son
- The son is called "natural son" — a legal term that could mean legitimate OR could distinguish from adopted/step children

**"Natural son" in 17th-century legal usage:**
- Most common meaning: legitimate biological son (as opposed to a son-in-law or stepson)
- Less common but possible: born out of wedlock
- The deed context suggests legitimate — he's receiving the family homeplace

**Tag:** 🟡 PLAUSIBLE — "natural son" = legitimate biological son. Hold open for alternative readings.
