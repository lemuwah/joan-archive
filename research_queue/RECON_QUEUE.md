## Bias check (required by Task 6)

**What this queue is strong on:** digitized English-language colonial land/court/probate records in RI/CT/MA.

**What it is still weak on, and where the weakness is now at least named:**

- **Indigenous-authored and community-governed sources** — one item (R47) with an explicit protocol gate; this is a relationship to build, not a database to query. Barrier honestly recorded.
- **Material culture / archaeology** — R48 only. No excavation data, no site survey in hand.
- **Irish/Scottish/origin-side records** — R38–R40 are listed but no archive member has worked these repositories; expect false starts.
- **Women's and household records** — R24, R50; the field itself is thin by design of the record-keepers, which is the phenomenon under study.
- **Maritime/port records** — R41; no Newport/Boston port-book sweep has ever been run by this archive.
- **Scribe identification** — R36/R49 are the archive's own records' weakest metadata; Ring 0 of the scribes list is entirely unsolved.
