## Institutional Barrier (Researcher-Confirmed)

The researcher has already contacted custodial institutions requesting scans. Every institution:
- Sent the **text transcription** (typeset) rather than a manuscript image
- **Declined to digitize** the original pages, citing limited staffing and fragile page condition

This closes the remote-request path entirely. The mark is not lost — it is inaccessible to remote researchers.

## What This Tells Us

- No publicly accessible digitized image of Joan's mark exists online.
- The mark almost certainly survives in physical/microfilm form.
- The gap is not in the record's existence but in **institutional capacity to digitize fragile 17th-century documents for individual requests.**
- This is a systemic problem, not specific to Joan — thousands of colonial-era marks, signatures, and manuscript details are locked behind the same barrier.
