## 🔴 PRIORITY 1: PAWTUXET DEED VERIFICATION (Blocks Archive Integrity)

The May 19, 1682 Pawtuxet deed (John & Joan Greene → William Carpenter) is cited as "RI Land Evidence, vol. VII, pp. 177–178." But:
- Bartlett's printed *Records of the Colony of Rhode Island* Vol. VII covers 1770–1776 — WRONG CENTURY
- RI State Archives "Land & Public Notary Records" Vol. VII = maritime/notary — NOT land deeds
- Worthington's RI Land Evidences Vol. I abstracts (1648–1696) — deed NOT FOUND
- The verbatim text has NEVER been read by anyone working on this archive

The citation "vol. VII" likely refers to a TOWN-LEVEL deed book (Providence or Warwick) or an unpublished manuscript volume — NOT Bartlett's printed series. Until the deed is located and read, Joan has **1 confirmed** legal appearance, not 2.
