## Priority Notes

- Items 1-4 are about **John's status** — was he a free settler, a servant, or something else?
- Items 5-10 are about **Joan's entry point** — if she's Irish, how did she arrive?
- Items 11-14 are about **the community** — who else was there, and where did they come from?

## The Status Quo We're Testing

The received narrative: John Greene is an English settler who married an English wife named Joan. **Neither assumption has primary source support.** Both need to be earned through evidence, not inherited from 19th-century compilations that assumed English origin by default.

If John is Irish-origin (Y-DNA), Model C for Joan goes from "open but unresearched" to "actively plausible and testable."

If John is a servant ("lived with Smith"), then two undocumented people marrying each other is the expected pattern, not an anomaly.
