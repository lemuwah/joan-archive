# Esther Smith — Identity Triangulation via Barton/Burnell Network

**Status:** 🟡 RESEARCH LEAD — no maiden surname proven; primary-source relationship network identified

**Research question:** Who was the woman identified by Richard Smith Jr. as his "cousin Ester Smith," and what was her documented relationship to William Barton of Morton, Gloucestershire?

---

## Primary Source Evidence

### The 1690/91 Will (Richard Smith Jr.)
- 🟢 **Verified:** Names "my wife Ester Smith" as executrix with lifetime possession of the estate
- 🟢 **Verified:** No surviving children — estate passes to Esther, sister Elizabeth (Viall/Newman), and nephews/nieces (Thomas Newton, Updike kin)
- **Probate:** Boston, Suffolk County, MA — probated 12 July 1692

### The 1669 Correspondence (Smyth Family Papers, University of Chicago)
- 🟢 **Verified:** Richard Smith Jr. wrote to his "unkell, William Barton, Morton, Gloucestershire"
- 🟢 **Verified:** Richard referred to "his cousin, Ester Smith"
- 🟢 **Verified:** Esther sent her love to "her uncle"
- **Note:** The pronoun in "her uncle" needs manuscript verification — the catalog description renders it as Esther's uncle, but the original must be checked directly before building genealogy on the pronoun.

### The 1665–1684 Correspondence (Winthrop Papers)
- 🟢 **Verified:** Esther was at Cocumscussoc/Narragansett from at least 1665
- 🟢 **Verified:** Esther personally wrote to John Winthrop Jr.
- 🟢 **Verified:** Richard asked Winthrop for treatment for his wife (1665)

### The Relationship Triangle

Richard Smith Jr. → "unkell William Barton" (1669 letter)
Richard Smith Jr. → "cousin Ester Smith" (1669 letter)
Esther Smith → sends love to "her uncle" (1669 letter)

This is a three-person relational triangle with documentary kinship language connecting them.

### Critical Methodological Point
**Joan's absence from William's 1625 will ≠ Joan was not his daughter.** Eleanor's 1645 will independently proves Joan was their eldest daughter. Therefore: Esther's absence from the 1625 will cannot be used as proof she was not William's child. This is a control case.

## Hypothesis Tree

### H1 — Esther was William Barton's daughter
- **Status:** 🟡 still possible
- **For:** Richard calls her his cousin; William is Richard's uncle; Esther sends affection to William; Joan proves daughters could be absent from William's will
- **Against:** Esther is absent from the known child reconstruction; no document identifies an Esther Barton
- **Verdict:** TESTABLE, not proven

### H2 — Esther was a daughter of one of William's siblings
- **Status:** 🟡🟡 very worth testing
- **For:** Would naturally explain Richard Jr. → cousin → Esther AND Esther → uncle → William; genealogically elegant
- **Against:** No identified sibling of William Barton connecting Esther; William's parents and siblings not yet reconstructed
- **Key search:** Reconstruct William Barton's parents and siblings from Thornbury/Moreton probate and parish records

### H3 — Esther was related through the Burnell side
- **Status:** 🟡 surprisingly interesting
- **For:** Eleanor's will reveals substantial Burnell network (sister Elizabeth, Winifred Burnell, Martha Burnell, William Burnell, John Burnell); if Esther came through a Burnell collateral line, the cousin relationship is explained
- **Against:** No direct Esther → Burnell document

### H4 — Esther was born Smith
- **Status:** 🟡🟡 must remain open
- **For:** "cousin, Ester Smith" could mean she was Smith by birth; the Smyth Papers acknowledge uncertainty in the broader Gloucestershire Smith connections; John Smith of Nibley is in the correspondence network
- **Against:** No specific Smith-by-birth identification found

## Contamination Kills (Negative Results)

- 🔴 **Esther = Mount** — No primary evidence found. Unproven surname insertion.
- 🔴 **Esther = Fosdick** — The documented Esther Fosdick in the Updike family is Lodowick Updike's daughter (married Thomas Fosdick, 1720). This is a next-generation conflation that migrated backward through online trees.
- 🔴 **Esther = Updike** — Lodowick Updike was Richard Jr.'s nephew and co-executor. Estate relationship ≠ birth identity.
- 🔴 **Esther = Long Island "Esther Smith"** — Richard "Bull" Smith of Smithtown family. No documentary bridge to the Narragansett Esther, whose footprint runs 1665–1691 at Cocumscussoc/Wickford. False merge — same pattern as Joan/Beggerly.

## Connection to Joan Greene

### What this does NOT establish
- ❌ Joan Greene = Esther Smith — **contradicted by simultaneous documented marriages** to different men in neighboring Narragansett households (Joan → John Greene, 1682; Esther → Richard Smith Jr., 1690/91 will)
- ❌ Joan Greene = Joan Barton — already ruled out (Joan Barton = wife of Richard Smith Sr., died before 1664)

### What IS worth testing
- 🟡🟡 **Joan Greene belonged to the same wider Narragansett kin/social network** — Quidnessett and Cocumscussoc/Wickford are neighboring locations in the same Narragansett political landscape
- 🟢 **Joan Greene's unknown identity may be recoverable through the Barton/Burnell/Smith collateral network** — this is now a legitimate research strategy
- The question is NOT "was Joan actually Esther" — it's: "Did the unidentified Joan Greene of Quidnessett belong to the same Barton/Burnell/Smith Gloucestershire kinship network that produced Esther Smith?"

### Falsifiable prediction
If Joan Greene came from this network, we should eventually find a documentary bridge: Barton, Burnell, Smith, a collateral Gloucestershire family, correspondence, migration record, witness relationship, land transaction, kinship terminology, or a later family record preserving the relationship. If we find nothing after systematic examination, the hypothesis weakens.
