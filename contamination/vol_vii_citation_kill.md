# Vol. VII Citation Kill — 2026-09-01
## Bartlett "Records of RI & Providence Plantations" Vol. VII pp. 177–178 = Gaspee Commission 1773

**Status:** ⚫ NULL — citation definitively invalidated
**Vector:** Source misattribution (volume/page confusion across different record series)
**Date identified:** 2026-08-31 (Kimi analysis); confirmed 2026-09-01
**Law applied:** Law 4 (No Algorithmic Contamination)

## What Happened

The Joan Archive source spine and 1682 deed lifecycle matrix cited:
> "Records of the Colony of Rhode Island and Providence Plantations in New England, Vol. VII, pp. 177–178"

as the source for a Pawtuxet deed dated 19 May 1682 (John Greene → William Carpenter, £10, Joan's consent).

**This citation is impossible.** Bartlett's Vol. VII covers **1770–1776** (published 1856). Pages 177–178 contain material from the **1773 Gaspee commission** — specifically, a request by Chief Justice Frederick Smythe dated Newport, June 23, 1773, and the commissioners' covering letter to Lord Dartmouth dated June 22, 1773, signed by J. Wanton, Fred. Smythe, Dan. Horsmanden, and Robt. Auchmuty.

No 17th-century deed exists at this citation.

## How It Got There

The likely vector is confusion between two different numbering systems:
- **Bartlett Colony Records** = 10 printed volumes (1856–1865), legislative/administrative records
- **RI Land Evidence manuscript volumes** = separate series at RI State Archives, numbered independently

Someone (possibly an AI, possibly a secondary compiler) mapped a Land Evidence manuscript volume number onto the Bartlett series, producing a citation that looks authoritative but points to the wrong century.

## Additional Finding: "RI Land Evidence Vol. IV"

The Kimi analysis also cited "RI Land Evidence Vol. IV" for the March 1682 home-place deed. RI State Archives describes Vol. IV as covering **1721–1741** (public-notary and maritime records). A March 1682 deed cannot be in Vol. IV. The correct source for a 1682 land deed is **RI Land Evidence Vol. I (1648–1696)** or a town deed book.

## Impact

- Every citation of "Vol. VII pp. 177–178" in the Joan Archive is invalidated
- The Pawtuxet deed's existence is now a matter for physical archive verification, not printed source retrieval
- This has been corrected in: README.md, source_spine.md, 1682_deed_lifecycle.md, proof piece #9

## Resolution Path

The Pawtuxet deed may still exist in:
1. RI Land Evidence Vol. I manuscript (FamilySearch Film 564389)
2. North Kingstown town records
3. Peirce Manuscripts (FamilySearch films 22291–22292)
4. An unpublished manuscript that was never assigned to a printed volume

It does NOT exist at Bartlett Vol. VII pp. 177–178.
